# VISCERIUM commenting policy

VISCERIUM comments are for discussion of the Codex, its stories and its world. Disagreement, criticism, strong language and mature discussion are welcome; using the comment system to target people, impersonate authority or distribute harmful material is not.

## Rules

Do not post spam or advertising, malicious or deceptive links, harassment or targeted abuse, threats, personal information that is not yours to share, impersonation of another person or VISCERIUM staff, or material whose primary purpose is to disrupt the page or other readers. This includes pathological Unicode/invisible-character payloads and other text crafted to damage or obscure the interface.

User-uploaded images, files, embedded media and affiliate/shortened redirect links are not supported. Ordinary external links are allowed subject to automated safety checks and conservative link limits.

Display names are not unique and do not establish authority. Staff status is derived from the authenticated internal identity, not from a chosen name. Reserved and confusable staff/system names may be rejected even when no malicious intent is established.

## Moderation

Moderators may remove comments, block accounts, place a thread or the entire service into read-only mode, or apply temporary anti-abuse controls when necessary. Reports are evidence for review rather than votes that automatically remove content. Repeated false or abusive reporting may itself be rate-limited.

Moderation should be proportionate to behaviour. Shared IP addresses, VPN use or similar network signals are not proof that two users are the same person and should not be presented publicly as such.

Where practical, significant moderation actions should retain an internal audit record containing the action, actor, target, timestamp and reason. The audit record is operational data, not a public dossier.

## Appeals and corrections

If you believe a moderation action was made in error, use the site's contact route and identify the affected comment or account without sending passwords, OAuth secrets or session cookies. VISCERIUM may correct or reverse a moderation action when the available evidence supports doing so.
