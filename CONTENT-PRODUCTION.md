# Content & Production Statement

VISCERIUM uses a range of tools to support its technical development. This may include AI-assisted tools for website code, site features, troubleshooting, automation, and customising our Obsidian workspace.

These tools do not create the world of VISCERIUM. No generative AI has been, or will be, used to produce its creative content. All articles, stories, lore, characters, places, artwork, and other published creative material are conceived and made by people.

Technology helps us build the framework; the work within it remains human.
