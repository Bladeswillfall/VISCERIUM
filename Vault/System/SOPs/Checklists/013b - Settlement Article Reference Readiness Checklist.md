# Settlement Article Reference Readiness Checklist

> **Parent SOP:** [[013 - Settlement Article Authoring SOP]]

## Support systems

- [ ] Water source, storage, distribution, and failure conditions are understood.
- [ ] Food supply, preservation, transport, storage, and reserve assumptions are understood.
- [ ] Fuel, power, sanitation, waste, housing, and construction materials support the claimed scale.
- [ ] Seasonal, wartime, outbreak, and infrastructure-failure conditions are documented where relevant.

## Movement and spatial logic

- [ ] Roads, rivers, coast, rail, air, tunnels, lifts, or orbital access explain the settlement's reach.
- [ ] Junctions, chokepoints, anchorages, extraction nodes, high ground, and defensive gaps shape the layout where applicable.
- [ ] District placement follows work, class, hazard, access, history, or defence.
- [ ] The settlement's relationship with its hinterland and dependent communities is clear.

## Institutions and population

- [ ] Population figures state model assumptions and confidence.
- [ ] Work, services, administration, security, and specialist labour agree with the population and era.
- [ ] Formal authority, practical authority, ownership, credit, justice, information, and protection can be traced.
- [ ] Marginalised groups and unequal access are visible where they affect the setting.

## Story support

- [ ] One supply dependency, one institutional conflict, and one physical vulnerability can produce consequences.
- [ ] Local customs and identity emerge from specific pressures or history.
- [ ] A creator can stage arrival, movement, daily life, crisis, defence, and departure without inventing foundational systems.
- [ ] Relevant research departures and VISCERIUM-specific causes are recorded.

## Decision

- [ ] **Reference ready**
- [ ] **Thin but usable** - named gaps recorded.
- [ ] **Not ready** - blocking foundational gap recorded.
