# Entity Article Publication Checklist

> **Parent SOP:** [[Entity Authoring SOP]]
>
> Use this checklist for fauna, flora, fungi, items, and similar structured records.

## Identity and use

- [ ] The opening identifies the subject quickly.
- [ ] The article states at least one distinctive feature or consequence.
- [ ] A creator can use the subject without inventing its defining behaviour.
- [ ] The development level matches the article's actual usefulness.

## Placement and consistency

- [ ] Era, location, biome, faction, or availability claims are established rather than guessed.
- [ ] Unknown optional properties are absent instead of set to false or none.
- [ ] Significant era changes use separate editions when required.
- [ ] The article does not contradict linked canonical notes.

## Structure and links

- [ ] Each structured property supports a real decision.
- [ ] Ordinary defaults are not documented only to fill space.
- [ ] Meaningful related subjects are linked.
- [ ] Structured relationships are reserved for relationships that matter independently.

## Presentation

- [ ] The prose is readable and free from publication-blocking placeholders.
- [ ] Images, frontmatter, and Storyteller material pass their relevant checklists.
- [ ] The note passes Vault Doctor after structural changes.

## Decision

- [ ] **Publication ready**
- [ ] **Not ready** - blocking reason recorded in the article task list or review note.
