# Settlement Article Publication Checklist

> **Parent SOP:** [[013 - Settlement Article Authoring SOP]]

## Location and scale

- [ ] The article gives one clear reason the settlement exists at this location.
- [ ] Fresh water, food, and essential access routes are identified.
- [ ] The stated scale agrees with its worst constraint or records a deliberate exception.
- [ ] Numerical population claims include a source or documented assumption.

## Function and layout

- [ ] The settlement has a clear economic, logistical, political, defensive, ritual, or historical job.
- [ ] Important districts follow terrain, movement, work, status, water, waste, or defence.
- [ ] Formal and practical authority are identifiable.
- [ ] Major services and unequal access are explained where relevant.
- [ ] At least one visible adaptation or historical scar affects the settlement.

## Dependency and pressure

- [ ] At least one important import, route, resource, institution, or external relationship is traceable.
- [ ] The article explains what happens when the main constraint or dependency fails.
- [ ] Local culture differs meaningfully from a copied national summary.
- [ ] At least one local tension can create consequences or story.
- [ ] The reasons to arrive and leave are understandable.

## Presentation

- [ ] Relevant parent regions, routes, maps, factions, and neighbouring settlements are linked.
- [ ] Frontmatter, images, Atlas material, and Storyteller content pass their relevant checklists.
- [ ] The note passes Vault Doctor after structural changes.

- [ ] **Publication ready**
- [ ] **Not ready** - blocking reason recorded.
