# Checklist Index

> **Next action:** Open the checklist linked from the parent SOP after the article work is complete.
>
> **Result:** You verify observable outcomes without repeating the authoring procedure.

## Rules

A checklist tests the result.

Do not use a checklist as a substitute for the parent SOP.

Complete required sections for every applicable article.

Complete a conditional section only when the article makes the related claim.

Use **Not applicable** only with a short reason.

## Completion record

Copy this block into a project note when the review needs a durable record:

```markdown
- Checklist:
- Article:
- Completed by:
- Reviewed by:
- Date:
- Readiness decision:
- Blocking findings:
- Deferred non-blocking findings:
```

Do not store completed check marks in the reusable master checklist unless the checklist itself is under review.
