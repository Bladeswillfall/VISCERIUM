# Political Character Publication Checklist

> **Parent SOP:** [[014 - Political Character Authoring SOP]]

## Role and ambition

- [ ] The character's institutional obligations are distinct from personal ambition.
- [ ] The institution or power centre is linked and has a clear goal.
- [ ] The character's influence comes from named resources, relationships, authority, information, reputation, or force.
- [ ] A credible limit prevents unlimited action.

## Thinking and contradiction

- [ ] The character's way of processing information affects decisions.
- [ ] One cognitive strength creates a practical advantage.
- [ ] One cognitive limitation creates a predictable class of mistake.
- [ ] A hidden belief, desire, fear, loyalty, dependency, or past action threatens the public role.
- [ ] Exposure has a concrete cost.

## Relationships and crisis

- [ ] Public and private alliances have understandable benefits and risks.
- [ ] At least one person or group distrusts the character for a specific reason.
- [ ] One plausible crisis forces duty, ambition, cognition, and contradiction into conflict.
- [ ] The likely choice produces a consequence for other people or institutions.

## Presentation

- [ ] The article does not declare importance without showing leverage.
- [ ] Structured relationships are used only where the relationship matters independently.
- [ ] The note passes frontmatter and publication checks.

## Decision

- [ ] **Publication ready**
- [ ] **Not ready** - blocking reason recorded.
