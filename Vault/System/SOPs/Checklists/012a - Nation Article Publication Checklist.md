# Nation Article Publication Checklist

> **Parent SOP:** [[012 - Nation Article Authoring SOP]]

## Identity and scope

- [ ] The article identifies the polity, era, territory, and defining feature.
- [ ] The article distinguishes public identity from practical reality where necessary.
- [ ] The government label is supported by an explanation of how power works.

## Place, people, and systems

- [ ] Geography creates at least one meaningful constraint or opportunity.
- [ ] Population and settlements have a plausible source of food, water, movement, and essential materials.
- [ ] Important resources have identifiable owners, routes, dependencies, or bottlenecks.
- [ ] Formal authority and practical leverage are distinguishable.
- [ ] Culture is connected to pressures, history, institutions, or belief rather than presented only as aesthetics.

## History and tension

- [ ] Major historical material explains a present consequence.
- [ ] At least one internal tension can produce change or story.
- [ ] Benefits and costs of the current system fall on identifiable groups.
- [ ] Neighbouring polities, predecessor states, major institutions, and important locations are linked where relevant.

## Evidence and presentation

- [ ] Numerical claims have a source, calculation note, or explicit assumption.
- [ ] Research baselines are not presented as automatic canon.
- [ ] Unresolved contradictions are visible tasks rather than silent choices.
- [ ] Frontmatter, images, and Storyteller material pass their relevant checklists.
- [ ] The note passes Vault Doctor after structural changes.

## Decision

- [ ] **Publication ready**
- [ ] **Not ready** - blocking reason recorded.
