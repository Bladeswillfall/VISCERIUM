# Nation Article Reference Readiness Checklist

> **Parent SOP:** [[012 - Nation Article Authoring SOP]]
>
> Complete this deeper audit only when a story, sourcebook, collaborator, or continuity decision needs it.

## Geographic and logistical model

- [ ] Important settlements, routes, waterways, chokepoints, surplus zones, extraction areas, and logistical shadows form a coherent system.
- [ ] Critical imports and exports can be traced from source to consumer.
- [ ] At least one supply failure has documented political, economic, or social consequences.
- [ ] Military, administrative, and trade reach agree with transport and communication conditions.

## Population and economy

- [ ] Population claims record their assumptions and level of confidence.
- [ ] Urban scale agrees with food, water, fuel, sanitation, housing, transport, and storage or records a deliberate exception.
- [ ] Major industries have plausible material, energy, labour, and knowledge requirements.
- [ ] Wealth creation, taxation, extraction, debt, and unequal access have identifiable beneficiaries and costs.

## Politics and institutions

- [ ] Every major power centre controls something specific.
- [ ] Authority has an identifiable basis of acceptance, dependence, legitimacy, or force.
- [ ] The visible and hidden currencies of power are understood.
- [ ] At least one fracture point and likely cascade are established.
- [ ] Important offices and political characters have concrete leverage and limits.

## Culture and history

- [ ] At least two defining cultural practices trace to a material, historical, or philosophical cause.
- [ ] Major values affect several areas of life.
- [ ] Cultural ideals and actual behaviour are distinguishable.
- [ ] Value tensions create consequences rather than remaining decorative contradictions.
- [ ] Historical events explain current institutions, borders, relationships, or behaviour.

## Security and continuity

- [ ] Armed force and security claims include supply, command, recruitment, endurance, and political constraints.
- [ ] Myrkild, Naranor, Resonance, and other VISCERIUM pressures are integrated where relevant.
- [ ] Cross-era developments identify what persists, changes, breaks, or is reinterpreted.
- [ ] A future writer can make the next major decision without inventing a foundational system.

## Provenance

- [ ] External frameworks, heuristics, calculations, and visual references are attributed.
- [ ] Evidence strength is recorded where the source could be mistaken for authority.
- [ ] Altered assumptions are visible beside dependent calculations.
- [ ] Original VISCERIUM additions are distinguished from the research baseline.

## Decision

- [ ] **Reference ready**
- [ ] **Thin but usable** - named gaps recorded.
- [ ] **Not ready** - blocking foundational gap recorded.
