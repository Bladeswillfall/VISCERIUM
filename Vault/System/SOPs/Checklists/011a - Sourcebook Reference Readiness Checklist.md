# Sourcebook Reference Readiness Checklist

> **Parent SOP:** [[Sourcebook Readiness SOP]]

## Product scope

- [ ] The hypothetical product, era, geographic scope, and audience are defined.
- [ ] The audit covers only world functions that the product needs.
- [ ] Each finding is labelled Strong, Thin, Missing but valuable, or Safe to invent later.
- [ ] Optional detail has not become automatic work debt.

## Place and movement

- [ ] A writer can locate the subject and trace important connected places.
- [ ] Entry, movement, exit, routes, seasonal changes, and hazards are understood where relevant.
- [ ] Important fauna, flora, fungi, threats, and environmental conditions can be placed without invention.

## People and systems

- [ ] Ordinary life has enough support to explain food, shelter, work, movement, and basic institutions.
- [ ] Formal and practical authority are distinguishable.
- [ ] Important resources, services, technologies, and dependencies can be traced.
- [ ] Social hierarchy and unequal access appear where they affect the product.

## History and pressure

- [ ] Relevant historical events still affect present behaviour or institutions.
- [ ] Outsider misunderstandings and local assumptions are identifiable.
- [ ] At least one credible tension can generate product material.
- [ ] The next product decision needs no missing foundational fact.

## Provenance

- [ ] Quantitative assumptions have visible sources or calculation notes.
- [ ] External frameworks are attributed according to [[Documentation Writing Standard]].
- [ ] VISCERIUM departures from the research baseline are recorded.
- [ ] Uncertain material is labelled rather than presented as settled canon.

## Decision

- [ ] **Reference ready**
- [ ] **Thin but usable** - named gaps recorded.
- [ ] **Not ready** - blocking foundational gap recorded.
