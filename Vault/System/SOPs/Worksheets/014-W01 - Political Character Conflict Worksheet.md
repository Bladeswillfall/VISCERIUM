## Sources and adaptation notes

### Primary source

- [[System/References/Source Notes/SRC-001 - The Grainbound Worksheets|SRC-001 - The Grainbound Worksheets]]
- Use in this worksheet: institutional goals, personal ambition, cognitive architecture, hidden contradiction, relationship pressure, and crisis testing.
- Relationship: adapted framework and practical synthesis.

The worksheet retains the four-layer conflict principle while reorganising it around concrete leverage, counters, consequence cycles, and canon transfer.

## Original VISCERIUM additions

- Requires every political capability to have a limit and rival countermeasure.
- Adds Resonance and unusual capabilities without allowing them to replace institutional leverage.
- Connects the crisis to beneficiaries, costs, institutional response, and the next decision.
