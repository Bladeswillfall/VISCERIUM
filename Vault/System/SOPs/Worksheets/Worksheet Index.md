# Worksheet Index

> **Next action:** Choose the worksheet that answers the unresolved authoring question.
>
> **Result:** You develop or stress-test the worldbuilding without turning every prompt into a publication requirement.

## Rules

Worksheets are optional unless the parent SOP identifies a current need.

A worksheet must record assumptions, contributors, and external sources.

A calculation is an estimate until the result is deliberately adopted in an authoritative note.

Do not copy a third-party worksheet into VISCERIUM. Adapt the method, rewrite the prompts, and explain the changes.

## Required end sections

Every worksheet must end with:

1. **Completion record**
2. **Assumptions and confidence**
3. **Departures from the research baseline**
4. **Sources and adaptation notes**
5. **Original VISCERIUM additions**
