# Notes
## The "Cradle" System
### Level 1: Hostile
**Extreme Conditions** - *Barren biomes*
Minimal water, poor soil, short growing season. Agriculture is impossible.
### Level 2: Severe
**Difficult but survivable** - Arctic/Desert biomes*
Seasonal water, thin soil, low biodiversity.
### Level 3: Moderate
**Seasonal environments** - *Badland/Mountain/Plains biomes*
Gathering and hunting produce enough of a surplus for semi-permanent camps.
### Level 4: Favourable
**Reliable environments** - *Wetland/Lowland/Tableland biomes*
Agriculture is viable, though it takes work - clearing, irrigation, fallow rotation.
### Level 5: Gentle
**Abundant environments** - *Lush biomes*
Abundant water, deep alluvial soil, long growing seasons.

## How population interacts with the "Cradle" System
### Level 1: Hostile
**Mobile bands** - *Nomadic (15 - 50 people)*
No permanent structures, no fixed territory.
### Level 2: Severe
**Difficult but survivable** - *Semi-Nomadic (30 - 80 people)*
Bands gathering seasonally into the low hundreds when resources allow, temporary shelters.
### Level 3: Moderate
**Seasonal environments** - *Semi-Permanent (50 - 200 people)*
People stay most of the year, long term storage of foodstuffs and resources.
### Level 4: Favourable
**Reliable environments** - *Permanent (200 - 2,000 people)*
Farming is now the dominant origin of food, land ownership.
### Level 5: Gentle
**Abundant environments** - *Permanent (5,000 - 100,000+)*
Social strata appears, farmers support specialist trades, complex bureaucracies appear.
