# Notes
This video isn't necessarily about anything in particular that I like/dislike. It's more about the creator in general and their approach/methods/storytelling ability. KITBASH CHAOS has a unique approach to building characters and lore around their models. They seem to repeatedly make in-depth well rounded groups of people with interesting characters and unique designs. They expertly build the lore into the apperance of the group and this is something I want to try and carry forwards.

Their "Chaos Bretonnian Knights" is another expert example of this same philosophy and approach. They are able to create such incredible stories for each model and yet still make the group look cohesive as well as visibly referencing their lore/backstory.
