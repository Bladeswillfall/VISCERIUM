# SRC-002 - Build Kingdoms

## Source

Site: <https://buildkingdoms.wordpress.com/>

## Evidence role

Build Kingdoms is a practical worldbuilding synthesis.

Use it as a planning baseline for demographic scale, settlements, agriculture, professions, travel, trade, armies, government, and related pre-industrial systems.

## Suitable uses

- checking whether cities and armies have a supporting population;
- comparing settlement scale and distribution;
- identifying demographic and economic assumptions;
- generating questions for CITADEL and transitional SMOG societies;
- finding historical constraints that VISCERIUM can deliberately alter.

## VISCERIUM adaptation rules

1. Record the specific page or article when a value materially affects a decision.
2. Treat figures as heuristics unless stronger evidence supports them.
3. Record local terrain, climate, crop, transport, institution, era, and supernatural conditions.
4. Use ranges or qualitative bands when precision is unsupported.
5. Record the consequence of any VISCERIUM departure.

## Unsuitable claims

Do not cite the site alone as proof of one historically correct demographic or military value.

Do not transfer a generic medieval assumption unchanged into SMOG, NEARSIGHT, or ENTROPY.
