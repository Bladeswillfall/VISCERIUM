## Evidence role

These sources provide visual vocabulary and process inspiration.

They do not, by themselves, establish historical provenance, regional use, date, manufacture, effectiveness, or universality.

The first EN World source and the direct Reddit media link require manual attribution review before their content is described in detail.

## Suitable uses

- identifying equipment components and silhouettes;
- improving vocabulary for armour, shields, gauntlets, helmets, and layered protection;
- planning visual references for CITADEL or SMOG articles;
- inspiring workshop, ink, pigment, soot, paper, or scribal processes;
- identifying a claim that needs stronger historical or material evidence.

## VISCERIUM adaptation rules

1. Record the exact visual or process feature being used.
2. Record the post title, contributor, original creator, and underlying source when available.
3. Separate visual inspiration from historical proof.
4. Verify historical claims through stronger sources before presenting them as baseline facts.
5. Do not reproduce source images in VISCERIUM documents without permission.
6. Translate the visual vocabulary into original VISCERIUM design decisions.

## Attribution gaps

The direct Reddit media URL does not provide durable creator or post context.

Record it as unverified until the original post and creator are identified.

The ink-making post credits or displays an account, but the historical claim and original process provenance remain unverified.

The first EN World page was not reliably inspectable during source review and remains provisional.

## Unsuitable claims

Do not use these sources alone to prove that:

- a depicted item existed in a specific place or century;
- a process is ancient, universal, safe, or technically complete;
- a fantasy illustration is an archaeological reconstruction;
- VISCERIUM should reproduce the source design directly.
