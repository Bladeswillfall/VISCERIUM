# SRC-005 - Food Timeline

## Source

Site: <https://foodtimeline.org/>

## Evidence role

Food Timeline is a curated food-history reference and research index.

Use it to locate historical context, terminology, ingredients, preservation methods, dishes, and underlying publications.

Food history often contains disputed dates, evolving practices, and regional variation.

## Suitable uses

- checking whether an ingredient, preparation, or preservation method is a plausible baseline;
- comparing class, region, period, ritual, military, and maritime food practices;
- tracing a Food Timeline entry to its cited books or articles;
- developing CITADEL and SMOG food systems before applying VISCERIUM changes.

## VISCERIUM adaptation rules

1. Cite the specific Food Timeline entry when it materially affects a decision.
2. Follow the underlying source when the claim is important or disputed.
3. Record region, period, class, preservation, transport, and availability assumptions.
4. Distinguish first recorded evidence from actual origin or universal adoption.
5. Record VISCERIUM flora, fauna, fungi, Resonance, trade, or cultural changes separately.

## Unsuitable claims

Do not cite the homepage alone for a specific historical assertion.

Do not treat one recorded recipe or date as proof of universal availability.

Do not assume an Earth ingredient or process exists unchanged in VISCERIUM.

## Redistribution note

Summarise and cite the relevant entry.

Do not reproduce extensive source text, recipes, images, or bibliographies without permission.
