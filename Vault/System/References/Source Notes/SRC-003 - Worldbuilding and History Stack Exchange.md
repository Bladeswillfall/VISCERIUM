## Evidence role

These pages contain community analysis and research leads.

Individual answers can use different assumptions and can contradict one another.

Cite the specific answer and author when an answer materially affects a VISCERIUM decision.

Follow underlying publications or primary sources when the answer provides them.

## Suitable uses

- identifying variables that affect food production and carrying capacity;
- comparing overland travel assumptions;
- comparing ship performance under different routes and conditions;
- identifying the labour, season, supply, and political limits on military mobilisation;
- locating stronger sources for medicine and substances;
- preserving competing interpretations instead of creating a false universal value.

## VISCERIUM adaptation rules

1. Record the answer used, its author, and its assumptions.
2. Do not average incompatible answers only to obtain one number.
3. Prefer ranges and scenario tests.
4. Separate a research lead from the underlying evidence.
5. Record local VISCERIUM conditions and consequences.

## Unsuitable claims

Do not present a discussion page as scholarly consensus.

Do not cite the question page alone when one specific answer supplies the calculation or claim.
