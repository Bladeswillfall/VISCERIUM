## Evidence role

These resources are practical worldbuilding syntheses.

Use them to expose assumptions, trace consequences, generate questions, and build planning models.

Do not present their values as settled historical laws.

## Suitable uses

- resource source, ownership, processing, transport, and bottleneck analysis;
- fuel and industrial dependency;
- settlement location, water, food, route, and defensive logic;
- population and agricultural planning estimates;
- political power centres, currencies, and fracture points;
- culture generation and reverse-engineering;
- political character conflict;
- economic transition and concentrated portable wealth.

## VISCERIUM adaptation rules

1. Rewrite prompts and explanatory language.
2. Combine overlapping methods into fewer modular worksheets.
3. Record every borrowed constant, ratio, range, or formula beside its use.
4. Treat estimates as planning support until a canon decision is made.
5. Adapt assumptions by era.
6. Add Resonance, Myrkild, Naranor, supernatural risk, environmental damage, institutions, and cross-era effects where relevant.
7. Record the consequence of each departure from the baseline.

## Unsuitable claims

Do not use this source alone to prove:

- one universal travel range;
- one universal population density;
- one fixed military proportion;
- one historically correct industrial ratio for all places and periods;
- the historical authenticity of a VISCERIUM decision.

## Redistribution note

Attribute adapted frameworks and calculations.

Do not reproduce the original worksheets, tables, prompt sequences, or explanatory passages in the public repository without permission.
