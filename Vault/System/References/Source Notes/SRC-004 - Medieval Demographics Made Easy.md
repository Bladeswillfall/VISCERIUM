# SRC-004 - Medieval Demographics Made Easy

## Source

Document: *Medieval Demographics Made Easy*

Author: S. John Ross

Hosted copy: <https://gamingballistic.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Medieval-Demographics-Made-Easy-1.pdf>

## Evidence role

This document is a practical game-design synthesis.

It provides broad demographic ranges, settlement-generation procedures, and business-support values for fantasy worldbuilding.

Use it as a planning model and comparison point.

Do not present its figures as one authoritative account of medieval demography.

## Suitable uses

- testing broad settlement and regional population scale;
- comparing rural and urban assumptions;
- estimating whether a service or business could be supported;
- identifying demographic assumptions that need stronger evidence;
- creating rough CITADEL planning scenarios.

## VISCERIUM adaptation rules

1. Cite each borrowed ratio, range, or support value beside its use.
2. Record local terrain, agricultural system, transport, mortality, institutions, and era.
3. Prefer rounded ranges over precise totals.
4. Compare the result with other sources and the settlement's actual supply systems.
5. Treat generated values as estimates until an authoritative note adopts them.

## Unsuitable claims

Do not use this source alone to prove:

- a precise historical population;
- one correct population density for all climates and periods;
- one universal frequency for professions or services;
- the supportable population of a settlement with non-standard VISCERIUM systems.
