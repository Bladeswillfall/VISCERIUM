The larger the mass of an astral object, the stronger its connection to the Wyrdweave is. Therefore, black holes and neutron stars have the strongest connection, though stars, planets and moons (or satellites) do also have a connection to the Wyrdweave, with their decreasing mass, the connection grows weaker.   Though invisible, the Wyrdweave creates a web of veins that envelope all objects of significant mass. These veins are then connected to larger tendrils that reach out into the void, grasping for connection, like roots in soil.  

### Resonance Amplification

For Resonants, proximity to strong Wyrdweave veins significantly amplifies their abilities. These areas, known as "hot spots," are highly sought after by those who wish to enhance their Resonance powers. However, the increased exposure to exotic radiation in these locations also comes with heightened risks.  

### Exotic Radiation

The Wyrdweave emits a form of exotic radiation similar to that produced by [[Drafts/WorldAnvil Import/Law-Resonance-433]] users, but significantly amplified. This radiation is a double-edged sword: while it serves as a powerful amplifier for Resonants, prolonged exposure can cause damage to cells and DNA, posing significant health risks to those who harness its power.  

### Energy Source

The Wyrdweave serves as an immense source of energy, particularly at points where its "veins" intersect or near massive celestial bodies. By harnessing the exotic radiation it emits, advanced civilizations have developed technologies to tap into this cosmic power grid.  

### Cosmic Navigation

Space travellers have learned to use the Wyrdweave as a natural navigation system. By detecting and following these cosmic "currents", they can plot efficient courses between celestial bodies, revolutionizing interstellar travel.
