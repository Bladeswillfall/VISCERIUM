> Progressus per unitatem, unitatem per aequitatem  
> _Translated: Progress through unity, unity through equity_
> 
> — Motto of the Trans-Continental Socialist Confederation

  The **Trans-Continental Socialist Confederation**, often referred to as the **TCSC** or just _"the Confederation"_ is a cross-continental political and economical aliance of socialist nations. Mostly connected by land borders, the TCSC utilizes freight trains and cargo caravans to transport goods between nations. These huge locomotives and lengthy caravans boast some of the most heavily armoured and armed vehicles on [[Lore/Degel System/Errack]]. Empowering, the TCSC to have the most extensive and interwoven international trainlines across the globe.  

## History

### Unification

In a move to reject the ruling oligarchy of the previous centuries, the TCSC was formed after a revolution that took place in multiple nations.   The leaders of the revolutionaries formed new governments in their home nations and quickly began to network with their neighbouring nations to form trade deals and border agreements amongst other treaties, solidifying their positions in power.  
As these networks of various agreements between neighbours grew and there was desire to trade with those futher afield rather than just those whom they shared boders with, the Trans-Continental Socialist Confederation was formed. Without which, such treaties would not work as people and cargo needed to be transported through various nations for said agreements.  

### Power over land

As a combined industrial power-house, the TCSC project an extensive range of ground-based control thanks to their heavy freight trains, armoured caravans and large numbers of military ground vehicles.  

## Single currency

Adopted by almost every nation in the TCSC, Marks allow member nations to trade goods and services without the need to adjust pricing every time for the current exchange rates. This makes the signing of trade agreements and fluidity of industry much simpler and interconnected, bolstering the Mark as well as bringing the various member nations closer together, unifying the TCSC further.   By adopting the Mark, the shared currency helps to increase market competition and efficiency making for a better economy in addition to producing higher quality goods and services for both public and private markets alike.  

## Organisational structure

As a republic the TCSC is led by two heads, two seperate elections are required and this can often lead to a dichotomy of intentions by the Confederation. However, this division of power alows each individual to focus on one job and therefore is significantly more efficient and quicker to respond to the ever changing world.

### Two heads

No one person holds all of the power. The Trans-Continental Socialist Confederation is governed by two leaders, one who handles the internal affairs of economic policies, laws and governance. The other handles the external affairs of border policing, encouraging collaboration and aiding in the organising of combined military exercises, and keeping open channels with those outside of the TCSC.

#### External Affairs

The President of Frontier Defence for the TCSC, presides over external affairs and is elected via democratic vote by elected officials of the TCSC for a 4-year tenure.

##### International trade and relations

Responsible for the continuation and flexibility of fair trade between members of the TCSC

##### External diplomatic relations

Responsible for communication with non TCSC nations, the President of Frontier Defence has a responsibility to keep diplomatic channels open and that their is clear and consistent information available.

##### Border security

As Head of an international policing and security organisation,  
Head of information security and cyber threat protection

 

#### Internal Affairs

The President of Home States for the TCSC, presides over internal affairs and is elected via democratic vote by elected officials of the TCSC for a 4-year tenure.

##### Legislative Branch

The Legislative branch defines new laws and passes them on to Executive branch to be signed and made official for all members. It consists of one democratically elected member from every nation within the TCSC.

##### Executive Branch

The Executive branch signs the laws proposed by the Legislative branch into power or rejects them to be amended or scrapped.

##### Judicial branch

The Judicial branch of the TCSC upholds the law as defined by the Executive branch and decides is said laws are consitutional.
