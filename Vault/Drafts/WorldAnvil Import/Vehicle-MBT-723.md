REF: Merkava mk3 body. Maybe Chieftan/ T-80U? Want it to be curvy and organic but have a low profile, kinda like the Merkava mk2 (and mk2d) turret.   Lower (vertically shorter) hull side-profile than the ASTU equivelant. Intent is for TCSC tanks to be more armoured at the front but also heavily rely on terrain advantage to enable ambush attacks. Organic shape lends itself to camoflague and hiding amongst terrain. ASTU tanks are more evenly armoured all round and rely more on range/speed on account of technological advancements.   Tanks have a stowage crate for cammo netting that the tank crew can order for their environment and use at will. Crews strongly advised to gather materials from the local area to further enhance the camoflauge capabilities of thier vehicles. In urban environments, this could take the form of bricks, fence panneling, pallets, bushes, concrete/rubble... whatever works to obsurce the tank's profile.
