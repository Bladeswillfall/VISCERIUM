## Cities/Settlements

- Walled, dense, multi-levelled cities, smog common

- Ref: [altered carbon](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2261227/), [DOOM: Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal) (environments for outbreak areas)

- Frequent outbreaks at low levels near the walls

## Armour/Weapons

- Full-body armour
- Vac-suit
- Advanced HUD
- Aim assist, target-tracking rounds
- Laser/Railgun weaponry

[![](/uploads/images/d3d3eb540d3800a094e338cde2ebc8a3.png "Untitled-3_1.png")](/i/3922758 "Untitled-3_1.png")

## Vehicles/Support

- Surface to vacuum capable troop transports
- AI assisted pilots
- Highly mobile, smaller units

- SPGs, Artillery etc are usually reserved for city defence
