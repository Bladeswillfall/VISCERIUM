> Listen, if you ever come across a Concubus, you better run for your life. They'll seduce you with their androgynous looks and then wrap their tentacles around you, draining your life force while you're still begging for more. And that's if you're lucky. If they decide to ensnare you with their chains and hooks, you'll wish for death to come and save you from the torture. Trust me, you don't want to mess with one.

  A Concubus is a fearsome and seductive creature from the netherworld. Completing the triad of Succubi, Incubi and Concubi, it possesses both the alluring charms of a female and the dominating prowess of a male, making it a formidable adversary or an irresistible temptation to those who cross its path.   Though its gender may be ambiguous, its powers are undeniable. The Concubus can shape-shift at will, adjusting its form to suit its whim. It feeds on the life force of those it seduces, draining them of their vitality and leaving them weak and vulnerable - perfect for capturing, and saving for later fun.   Despite its demonic nature, there is a certain allure to the Concubus. It is said that its touch can bring unimaginable pleasure, and that those who fall under its spell will do anything to stay in its embrace.  

## Appearance

This androgynous creature has an intoxicating demonic appearance, with four arms and leathery tortured wings, skin that is almost transparently thin - allowing you to see the wine-red muscle and inky tendons beneath. Though almost transparent, its skin is smooth and flawless, with a faint sheen that seems to reflect the darkness around it. The Concubus also posesses sharp hooked claws, several thin prehensile suckered tentacles and eyes that seem to peer into the very soul of its victims.  

## Devious Demeanor

In contrast to its Succubi and Incubi cousins, the Concubus is a switch - capable of being both dominating and submissive. One benefit of being a switch is that the Concubus is more adaptable and versatile in its seduction of individuals. It is able to adjust its approach to fit the preferences of different targets, whether they prefer to be dominated or to dominate. This gives it an advantage over Succubi and Incubi, who are limited to one approach or the other.   However, being a switch also makes the Concubus less specialized in either role. It is not as skilled or effective at dominating as an Incubus nor as skilled or effective at seducing as a Succubus. This makes the Concubus less formidable than either of these more specialized creatures in certain situations.   Additionally, being a switch also makes the Concubus more unpredictable and volatile. Its behavior and actions are harder to anticipate, as it may switch between dominant and submissive behaviors depending on its mood, the situation, or the preferences of its target. This can make it a more dangerous opponent, but also more difficult to control or manipulate.  

## Traits

### Mastery of pleasure and pain

The Concubus is a master of pleasure and pain, capable of inflicting intense sensations on its chosen individual that blur the lines between pleasure and agony. It is inately capable of producing a liquid substance that upon contact with human skin causes an agonisingly intense burning sensation, almost like the skin itself is boiling, whilst simultaneously causing impesurable pleasure. This substance has caused muscle damage, seizures, cardiac arrest and in some cases permanent damage to the brain.  

### Collective consciousness

The Concubus has the ability to form a collective consciousness with its target. Upon its touching and subsequently inserting of a needle-like tendril into the nape, the Concubus is able to connect with its target and share their knowledge and experiences. This gives it a unique advantage in manipulating and controlling its victims, as well as adapting to different situations and opponents.
