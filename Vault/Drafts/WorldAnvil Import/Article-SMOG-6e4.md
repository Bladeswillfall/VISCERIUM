In this era marked by the throes of the Industrial Revolution, trench warfare and the cacophony of clanking mechanical machines define the landscape. The world is shrouded in billowing black smoke, the grim aftermath of ceaseless industry. Amid this industrial maelstrom, chemical weapons proliferate, becoming a grim and ubiquitous facet of warfare.   Classism is rife and proliferated by a historical detest of [[Drafts/WorldAnvil Import/Law-Resonance-433]]. Politics, Pacts and manipulation have kept the wealthy in power. Sorcerers are conscripted by default, regardless of age and serve on the frontlines, utilising their abilities to stay alive, bolstering those armed with bolt-action rifles and other tools of industrial warfare.   The weapons of humankind bear down upon the Myrkild hordes, their mighty adversaries now facing an onslaught of relentless industrial might. As the Industrial Age reshapes every facet of human life, battles between humanity and the Demons take on an increasingly gruesome and sporadic nature. Armoured machines now dominate the battlefield, their metallic behemoths unleashing devastation upon the enemy. Chemical weapons, wielding the power of science's darkest alchemy, stain the earth and sky with toxic horrors, leaving landscapes desolate and lifeless.   In a world embroiled in conflict, humanity’s ancient enemy stands at the precipice of newfound power. The crucible of industrialization has forever altered the nature of warfare, and as humanity strives to secure its survival, the price of progress is etched in the blood and anguish of battle.
