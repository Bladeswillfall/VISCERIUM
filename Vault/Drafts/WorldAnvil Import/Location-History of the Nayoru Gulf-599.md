### CITADEL

](#spoiler-2gT9bw)

The City of Kysara. Renowned for its large docks providing great access to and from the city. The docks backed onto a series of grand warehouses, market stalls and worker residents. A smaller dock upriver from the gulf is reserved for the cities higher echelon of people, providing both a route for guests and cargo alike.  
During the early years of this era, a large armoury was constructed. Located centrally to the outer wall, supplying it's defenders with many varietys of arms and armour.

### SMOG

](#spoiler-k7qlfE)

During this era, the citizens of Kysara began to expand. The old stone walls defending the city where torn down to make way for industrial growth. As the old walls where torn down, new ones where built, smaller and further afar, ensuring ample room to spread the fires of industry.  
During this great expansion, a new metro network was constructed. Including a large main rail that encircled the city for greater access and manuverability.  
In time, the industrial complex spread beyond the reaches of the newly developed metro lines and the new defensive walls now began to hinder progress, restricting the expansion of industry. As such, companies began to rally and lobby for new expansions, pressing further and further out. Each time, requiring a new section of wall to be built and the old parts destroyed. During this time of dangerous expansion, a Demon horde reared it's ugly head and struck where the defences where weakest in the newly developed industrial areas. What started as a small incursion of demonic fiends, lead to an eventual torrential flood of corruption. Demons quickly gained ground inside the city limits and destroyed the new factories, tearing down the walls and pillaging their remains.   [...] a great and lengthy war occured. Trenches stretched out for kilometers, scarring the land around the city as far as the eye could see. The use of strange chemicals and gases poisoned the atmosphere and torrents of acid rain were not uncommon.

### NEARSIGHT

](#spoiler-EjpInI)

After being confined to within their largest wall - [TBN], the people of Kysara City have had no choice but to build and live vertically. Grand skyscrappers and appartment blocks make up most of the city. Underground transport has flourished, expanding off of the old metro lines, as well as a layered network of roads and walkways above ground. Those who can, work from home and tend to enjoy a space filled with high-oxygen producing house plants. It's uncommon for most folk to leave their area of the city as almost everything can be found on one level or another within a few blocks or can be delivered at a small cost.
