Strain pressure: Lust Silhouette: Vast cup-shaped blooms, some wider than cottages, rising low from fens and warm valleys. In flowering season they colour entire basins in bruised pink, ivory, and arterial violet. What they really are: Thermal nectar-traps with a fleshy basin, filament-veils, and a shallow fluid core warmed by intense metabolic activity. How they compete: Seduction ecology. Their scent draws animals, insects, and people; their warmth creates local fog and microclimate; their barbed inner filaments feed through countless tiny abrasions. Lust in VISCERIUM works best as attraction weaponised into bodily harm, not cheap erotic nonsense. Behaviour: At night, the basins steam in cold weather. Whole lowlands can look like blushing wounds in the mist. Use: Distilled pollen is a painkiller, euphoriant, and ritual narcotic. Fear: Corpses found near Veil Basins often died smiling, which is exactly the kind of detail people remember and hate.
