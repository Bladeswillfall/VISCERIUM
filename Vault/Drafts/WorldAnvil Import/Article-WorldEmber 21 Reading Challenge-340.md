## Summary

There were simply some amazing articles produced by the WorldAnvil community this year during World Ember and I'm proud to say I was a part of it.   My main takeaways from the above articles that have inspired me, is to refine my user experience through sleek CSS as well as fully utilising BBcode commands to play with article layouts in a manner that breaks up content for the reader in an unintruisive, natural way. Drawing the reader's eye arround the page to various focal points to keep their interest.   In addition to this, going back and refining my articles to include emotive content such as quotes or simply including more storytelling elements where possible. Articles that do this stick in the readers mind and makes content more memorable.
