## Target Audience

VISCERIUM targets an **18+ audience** due to its mature themes.   There is no gender target, VISCERIUM is to accommodate as many individuals as possible. Whilst in tone, it may resonate with and engage the male gender more predominantly, VISCERIUM is to be inclusive in its content. VISCERIUM is for all of appropriate age.  

> Inspired by **occult and pagan themes**, with a drive to question worldviews of morality and balance. VISCERIUM targets a **mature audience** and aims to deliver a deep story that is sure to intrigue both **fantasy _and_ Sci-fi** fans alike with it's continuous history spanning four key eras.
> 
> — [Bladeswillfall](https://www.worldanvil.com/author/Bladeswillfall)

  Additionally, VISCERIUM is inspired by works of alternate-history that blend cultures together to form unique geo-politics, design languages and interesting conflicts. VISCERIUM finds its roots entangled with many fiction IPs, and yet aims to distinguish itself all the same.  

## General Tone

VISCERIUM has the capability to house any and all types of stories within it. To balance this and provide some consistency, all stories told within this universe should adhere to the meaning of VISCERIUM as defined above. In short, they should be gritty whilst also providing some comedic relief where appropriate. Laughter, shock and sometimes tears - Intense emotions are at the core of a VISCERIUM story.   VISCERIUM should not feel depressing, but instead feel unforgiving, ruthless, dynamic and complex. There is room for vibrant stories and wonder, room for love and heroism. But there is also room for devastation and sudden loss, room for small mistakes to have dire consequences.  

## Genre definitions

Genre is a funny thing, isn't it?  
There are so many variations of what fits in which box, and these definitions are widely disputed... But i'll give it a try all the same, VISCERIUM at its core could be described as **dark fiction**. This broader description allows for the flex that VISCERIUM needs with such a large scope.  
  There are elements of _grimdark_ and _gilded_ worldbuilding present in VISCERIUM, and as such it does not fit neatly into either description, so, the more ambigious genre of _dark fiction_ is what we shall go with.  

## What is VISCERIUM not?

### In genre, story?

For children, High fantasy, Hard science, Romantic Comedy, Slice of life...  

### In tone?

- **Morally clear-cut or simplistic**: _Resist portraying characters and conflicts in a black-and-white manner. Moral ambiguity and shades of grey should characterise the dilemmas faced by individuals within VISCERIUM, adding depth and complexity to the storytelling._
- **About escapism or wish fulfilment**: _Resist portraying the setting as a utopia or idealised fantasy realm where problems are easily solved or conflicts quickly resolved. Instead, embrace the harsh realities and challenges inherent to each era of VISCERIUM._
- **Devoid of hope**: _While the tone of VISCERIUM may be dark and oppressive, it should not be devoid of all hope. Characters may cling to fleeting moments of optimism or strive for a better future, even in the face of overwhelming odds._
- **Predictable or formulaic**: _Resist falling into clichés or predictable storytelling tropes. Keep audiences on their toes by subverting expectations and introducing unexpected twists and turns in the narrative._
- **Emotionally detached or apathetic**: _Ensure that the tone of VISCERIUM resonates with audiences on an emotional level, eliciting visceral reactions and engaging their empathy. Avoid narratives that feel detached or indifferent to the struggles and triumphs of the characters._
- **Overly fantastical or whimsical**: _While elements of the supernatural or occult may be present, avoid veering into fantastical realms that diminish the grounded and gritty tone of the setting. Maintain a balance between realism and mysticism to keep the tone consistent and immersive._
