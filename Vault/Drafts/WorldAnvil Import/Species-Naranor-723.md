> Your comprehension is a fleeting shadow, a momentary flicker in the vast expanse of our understanding.  
> We are the assemblage of countless Nareel, a collective consciousness that transcends your finite minds.   We have existed for millennia before you, and we will exist for millennia after you. We will repeat this experiment as many times as we need to. Our journey _will_ continue.   We _are_ the cycle.

  The Naranor are a highly-advanced species formed from a gestalt of worm-like organisms connected via bio-mechanical chemical pathways to form individual assemblages. They exist primarily at the fringes of the [Degel System](/w/viscerium/c/degel-system-category) and beyond, the black veil projected from their Cavea stations containing Humanity and the Myrkild within.   Throughout history, the Naranor have tipped the scales of reality in their favour and bent the threads of destiny to their whims. Intervening to correct Myrkild advancements in a more desireable direction, whispering in the ears of vulnerable humans to knee-cap the progression of [Resonants](/w/viscerium/a/resonance-article), and applying pressures to speed up the deveopments of their grand harvest.   Very little is known about the Naranor's full list of interventions in Human history, let alone the history of the Naranor themselves. From what is known, it is clear the Naranor have been in the Degel system for millennia, and that their technology has always far exceeded human understanding.  

## Manifestation of Angels

Nareel Houses, and Assemblages - what is a Naranor?  

### Nareel

These worm-like organisms, called ‘Nareel’ are capable of integrating themselves with complex machinery and devices enabling them to control, pilot and reconfigure non-organic mechanisms to their whims.   The Nareel excrete an organic compound that mutates the DNA of other living organisms, altering them to take on traits of the Nareel species, and make them more susceptible to the desires of the Nareel/Naranor assemblage.   Whilst the Naranor are more rigid and unable to adapt their form as significantly as the Myrkild, they do still retain the ability to convert members of their assemblage (the Nareel) into limbs, organs, tissue, feathers and any other facsimile of living materials that they desire through metamorphosis. This conversion is one of more permanence though as it disassembles the Nareel(s) to create the new limb or tissue that is required. As such, the Naranor tend to keep a creche from which they can draw new members into their assemblage.

[![](28bf5a340dd9b3fc2605f57037a5859e.png "Generic Nareel.png")](/i/6515970 "Generic Nareel.png")

  As more Nareel combine into a greater mass forming a larger assemblage, they grow in intellect. Each new addition adds more neurons to the collective local consciousness. Initially, singular Nareel have very limited inteligence as they are simply driven by pheremones, stimuli, and a desire to 'connect' with a living organism.  

### Assemblages

Assemblages are best defined as the being that is formed by a gestalt of Nareel, in other words, a Naranor. These gestalts bear individuality derived from their Nareel House. Assemblages have a form of local connected consciousness, each Nareel acts as a single neuron connected to the larger gestalt forming a singular conscience.

## The Zeniths

The four Zeniths, Vyrkurn, Elskyn, Solthain, and Rauthir have existed for untold millenia, consistently adding to and replenishing their number with more Nareel throughout the ages.  
As the founders of their great houses, together, they lead the collective of Nareel and Naranor asemblages throughout the Degel system.   Each Zenith is a monstrous creation of incredible scale. It is believed that Vyrkurn and Solthain are stationed together on Aquilo station, whilst Elskyn and Rauthir are onboard Auster. Given how the Naranor form assemblages, it is believed that the Zeniths stretch their forms throughout their stations, entwining with each system through grand networks of twisted tendrils and machines. This assumed knowledge comes from the deployment patterns and captured intel of Naranor vessels. Whilst not entirely reliable, it does seem to align to observed flight paths of tracked vessels.  

## Houses of the Naranor

### House of the Voidwound Spiral

Lead by Vyrkurn, the House of the Voidwound Spiral are recognisable by their black carapaces and armour, often accented by subtle colours. Most commonly associated with themes of; The void, entropy, decomposition, putrefaction, calcination, black holes, and death, The House of the Voidwound Spiral are feared for their ability to weaponise decay as a tactical tool, accelerating entropy to destabilise reality itself.  

### House of the Mercury Veil

Lead by Elskyn, House of the Mercury Veil are distinguished by their dark silver armour and ethereal prismatic sheen.  

### House of the Gilded Tribunal

Lead by Solthain  

### House of the Sanguine Lattice

Lead by Rauthir

The Naranor remained a myth throughout most of human history. So preoccupied by the Myrkild, the common people did not realise the ever increasing Naranor presence until the early _(not sure how early to go, maybe somewhere between the 2nd-8th?) century of the 10th millennium when rumours of messengers from the heavens began to spread amongst the religious folk. These “messengers” would later be found imprisoned within grand churches and mausoleums, turned into martyrs and worshiped.   By the 1st century of the 11th millennium, several notable figures had been discovered to have been host to an Uyilor organ, or Nareel parasite. These alien additions to their human bodies became widespread stories and all manner of tales would be told of witchery, corruption and blessings alike. During this same period, tales of Naranor strikes against the Myrkild would also spread. Beams of light from the sky turning hordes of Myrkild into nothing but glass, swathes of people suddenly behaving differently overnight from their usual selves, shiny metal flying creatures that would pass overhead faster than any known Myrkild construct, and even accounts of beams of fire slicing through cities just to target something on the other side.

By the 3rd century of the 11th millennium, religions based around the Naranor seemed to outpace the growth of Myrkild religions and as such found themselves entwined with leaders of villages, cities, and nations alike causing multiple religion-fuelled wars and skirmishes. Folk would begin hunting down Resonants on behalf of the Naranor. Attempts to dissect the Nareel of a deceased Naranor would occur but due to the rarity of Naranor corpses, any scientifically-minded folks would struggle to understand the complex nature of the Nareels composition, regularly miss-identifying organs and functions of the Nareel form. Where Nareel had been metamorphosed into limbs, tissue or organs for the Naranor assemblage, these were believed to have been harvested from other creatures and were not understood to have been formed by sacrificed Nareel.

In the 8th century of the 11th millennium, Resonants would be used as great weapons of war. Those who followed the Naranor religions would continue to enact witch-hunts and crusades against the Resonants during these wars to take advantage of the global distraction and further the Naranor’s agendas. By now, the common people were aware of the Naranor and knew that they were composed of Nareel, though still not fully understood, they could be distinguished from the Myrkild. During this century, folks would begin to accumulate a shared knowledge of the Naranor craft that tear through the clouds above through drawings, photographs and estimated measurements based on witness testimonies. This shared knowledge would also enable the beginning of a classifying system for the various craft and further humanity’s understanding of their capabilities.   During the beginning of the 9th century, Naranor religious folk would begin using small quantities of the excreted bio-chemical agent of the parasitic Nareel to formulate truth-serums.

By the 10th century of the 11th millennium, humanity is able to search the night sky with powerful telescopes and sensors. In this era, Naranor spacecraft are infrequently detected and even more rarely, seen in the void. This causes great concern within the scientific communities and is kept as a global secret by the national governments whom detect these craft. Over the next few years, the Nareel would be mostly understood by scientists, though not entirely as some functions still remain the subject of discussion. The metamorphosis process will be witnessed and also partly understood. Also during this era, scientists studying defeated Naranor craft would understand that they utilise an unknown power-source and significantly advanced control systems.   In the 1st century of the 12th millennium, a probe launched from Errack reaches one of the Naranor Cavea stations - previously believed to be one of the few stars in Errack’s sky. This discovery spreads across the globe like wildfire and causes mass panic. The world transitions into a new era for Humanity. Over the next few decades, the theory of the Naranor prison that encompasses the Degel system would be explored and folks would begin to question why the Myrkild are here, why humans are here, and why the Naranor have built a cage - is it to protect everyone? Is it to study us? Or something else entirely? In this same century, Dr.Adrastus Delroy derails the INTICO weapons facility in order to protect the Myrkild from its wrath - believing that the Myrkild will aid humanity to escape the grasps of the Naranor.

Within the 5th century of the 12th millennium, humanity has expanded into the void of space and frequent clashes occur between Naranor vessels and human ships. The Myrkild have also reached out into the void and complicate this new frontier even further. Naranor creches are discovered on other planets within the Degel system.

## How did the Naranor develop as a species?

Though we cannot know for certain how the Naranor came to be, nor the true extent of their civilization and capabilites, based upon centuries of research it is understood that the below is a logical framework for the timeline of Naranor advancement.  

### Initial Collective Behavior:

The Nareel begin to form small clusters due to environmental pressures, such as shared resources or predator avoidance. This initial grouping allows them to survive better and discover the benefits of collective action.  

### Discovery of Enhanced Intelligence:

As more Nareel join these clusters, they notice an increase in collective intelligence and problem-solving capabilities. This realization encourages them to intentionally merge and grow their assemblages.  

### Divergence and Parasitic Exploration:

A subset of Nareel diverge from the collective strategy and instead form parasitic relationships with other fauna on their planet. By controlling these hosts, they gain new insights into diverse environments and learn to adapt to different ecological niches. This divergence sparks innovation within the collective Nareel assemblages.  

### Metamorphic Adaptation and Tool Development:

Witnessing the adaptability of their parasitic counterparts, the Nareel assemblages begin to use their metamorphic abilities to create specialized limbs or organs. These adaptations allow them to manipulate and build simple tools, which eventually evolve into more complex machinery. These new forms are considered proto-Naranor.  

### Scientific Advancements:

As the collective grows, so does its intellectual capacity. The Naranor begin to understand and manipulate more advanced technologies, eventually leading to breakthroughs in physics and engineering necessary for space travel.  

### Space Exploration:

With sufficient technological advancements, the Naranor develop spacecraft capable of interstellar travel. Their adaptability enabling them to navigate the challenges of space exploration effectively.
