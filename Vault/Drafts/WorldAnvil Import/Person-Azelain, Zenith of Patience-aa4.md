## Patience events

The portal is a gently shimmering simple oval portal that has a silvery-blue tint and floats just above the surface of an endlessly quiet flowing pool of clear water that slowly drips over the edges of a clean, circular pool. The portal is surrounded by smooth plain pebble gravel encompassing larger smooth rocks. The gravel is raked to make rings around the portal and larger stones. As you approach the portal, gravity seemingly increases in perpetuity until you're trapped in place and can no-longer move forward.
