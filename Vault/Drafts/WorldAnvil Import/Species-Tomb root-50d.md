This plant has earnt it's name through the act of grappling and swallowing unsuspecting people and creatures alike. Once swollowed, the victim is quickly worked down through the stem and into the roots of the plant where the victim is likely to remain trapped within. Left to suffocate, the corpseses within the plant's roots give it plenty of nutrients to feed off of.   Unable to digest the bones of it's victims, the root remains stuffed and now useless to the plant. As such, this plant's lifespan is limited to the number of hollow roots it can produce whilst developing.
