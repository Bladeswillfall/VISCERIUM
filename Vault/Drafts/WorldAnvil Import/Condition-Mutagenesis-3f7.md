The condition wherein an organism is altered by the nearby presence of Myrkild cells and their corrupting properties. Not to be confused with infection, mutagenesis modifies the genetic properties of the organism creating divergent evolution at an increased rate. Mutation of the genetic structure has effected many flora and fauna across [[Lore/Degel System/Errack]] alike.   Mutations generally occur from prolongued exposure to neraby Myrkild and effect offspring most drastically.  
In rare cases, mutation can also occur when a parent is infected.
