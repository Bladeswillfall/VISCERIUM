Sub  
  The Succubi are truly dangerous and devious beings. Through the use of deception, seduction and manipulation, the Succubus is a great infiltrator and often works from the shadows. Dealing in favours and rewards for information.  
  Encounters with a Succubus often result in one of two things; pleasure, or pain. For those are what a Succubus enjoys most. Often, those two things are the same to a Succubus and why they're so dangerous.
