Standard issue bullpup pistol for section commanders and other senior personnel serving in the armed forces.   APBBC (Armor Piercing pen, Balled, Ballistic Capped) rounds APBBC is a specialised bullet that features an armour-piercing rod affixed to the ballistic cap, and a segmented jacket reminiscent of traditional hollow point bullets. Upon impacting a target, the armour-piercing dart enables the leaves of the hollow point to stay closed for longer before finally blooming and imparting the kinetic energy onto the target.  
APBBC rounds were specially designed to combat lightly armoured Myrkild constructs, using calibers too small to effectively carry a UVc payload.
