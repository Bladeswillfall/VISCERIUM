## Homework (June)

Each year, we fellow Anvilite's are invited to prepare for the upcoming month of July, and Summer Camp proper, by collectively doing some 'homework'. We're given [weekly assignments and guidance](https://www.worldanvil.com/community/challenge/summercamp-prep-2025/homepage) by the amazing staff at WorldAnvil to ensure that we're ready and able to have the best Summer Camp possible!

What happens when they don’t?"_   I think that this theme should be quite beneficial for VISCERIUM as I have lots of room for creativity here. #### Assignment 3: Reflect

**Task**: _"Find your earliest worldbuilding project. What mistakes did you make that you want to avoid? What good ideas from those early days can you integrate into your current project? Remember to take a moment to be proud of how far you've come!"_   Oof, my earliest worldbuilding project?... That's a tricky one as I have always been a bit of a dreamer. I've always looked at ways to integrate something/someone of my own creation into the stories and settings that I have loved since childhood.  
The most notable one that stands out as 'proper' worldbuilding I suppose though would be from a time when I was heavily into the Star Wars universe - obsessed with YouTube documentaries, lore dumps and watching any and all Star Wars content I could get my hands on...  
During this period of fixation, I created a character that mostly used the EU sources (because there is so much more content), and I attempted to weave them into the timeline of Star Wars, and with events that I found interesting. I did this by creating my own timeline of key events and describing what my character was doing/where they were. I didn't want this character to interfere with the story but rather carve out their own place in it. An example of this mentality would be that I recall having the character serve as one of Count Dooku's minor-bodyguards on Geonosis during the Petranaki arena sequence. This way, my character could be present, and yet not alter the timeline/outcome.   Is there anything I could integrate into my current project from this? Well, I suppose in a way I already have, as I have multiple crazy timelines tucked away that I am slowly transitioning onto WA for all you lovely folks. This theme of linear-interconnectivity has really driven VISCERIUM into what it is today.
