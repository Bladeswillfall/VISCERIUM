Across the globe devastating battles rage. From wide-open blackened fields scarred with trench lines and craters, to cramped urban combat within the desolate ruins of former cities, the fires of war burn ever bright.   Small groups of specialy trained soldiers often engage in guerilla warfare within demonic territories to cripple the progress of the Nadirs. They aim to acomplish this task by stealing crucial artefacts, destroying key targets, capturing units for research and rescuing captives.  

## Natural enviroments

### Fields of [TBC region]

Entrenched here are the defences of man, hoping to prevent any further incursion into Humanities territories. These fields are now more akin to a swamp or bog than any farmers pasture or natural reserve. Here, the wide open landscape has been convereted into a maze of layered trenches kilometers deep. Fortified weapon emplacements, barbed wire, mines, caltrops and anti-tank barriers are scattered over the surface. You'll seldom see a soldier cross over the trenches here. Instead, the use of drones and MLRS systems are the preferred method of engagement.  

### Forests of [TBC Region]

Amongst the rough woodland of these mixed forests, short to medium range warfare rages against the minions of Vanaer, Nadir of Pride. Toppled logs, stripped and decimated trees, bunkers and trenches makeup most of the notable features in this region.  
Here, the use of drones is less common due to the fast moving nature of Vanaer's kin and the densely covered treetops. Instead, the use of vehicles and smaller mecha are more frequent, opting for manuverability with strong firepower and decent armour.  
In addition to the more mechanized units here, a wide range of fortified emplacements protect and hold the line utilising a range of artilery, cannons and machine guns to allow Humanity's forward advances a _safe_ spot for respite.

## The lost cities

These dusty landscapes of once great cities now reduced to crumbling tower blocks and rubble are the site for many smaller conflicts. Typically infected with the creeping shadows of Evaxi, Nadir of Envy, these urban enviroments are a breeding ground for smaller demons able to swiftly traverse the crevices between structures and skulking assassins. Urban enviroments are extremely dangerous places to be as there are unending vantage points and no way of knowing for sure what is round the next corridor or hiding beneath the surface.  
Here, the armies of the Imperium Coalition prefer to make use of small, fast moving bipedal mechs, micro-drones and APC's or IFV's for transport. In addition to these ground forces, airspace is typicaly reserved for unmanned drone helicopters in support of the ground forces.  

### TBC City

Here, a stronger than usual presence of Sletair, Nadir of Lust, is presented through the obnoxious ammount of feremones in the surrounding air, the copious pools and puddles of milky white goo overflowing from overgrown tower blocks and the abundance of dangerous flora.  
Pity those who unknowinly wonder too close, for the seductive whispers and flirtatious laughter seem to echo every which way, luring prey ever inwards until a strange scream of terror reverberates once more among the high walls.  

## Demonic territories

Dependant entirely on which Nadir controls the area, Demon held teritory can vary vastly between the Nadirs and as such, each teritory should be approached in a different way.  

### Krathan

In the case of Krathan's teritory, this manifests as huge, skyward facing, visciously jagged rocks errupting from a shallow sea of bloody pools and rivers entwined with the bones and flesh of past victims. Spouts of blazing fires whip around the smoky skies and incinerate anything in their paths leaving nothing but infernos behind.  
Krathan's domain frequently contains a lot of crude iconography, traps and fortifications constructed by their lesser Demons, marking and protecting the territory of their all powerful leader.

### Boleth

In contrast, Boleth's teritory is slimey. Encompassed by damp, dead soil, stringy lumps of fat and writhing bloated maggots, dead flora and dead fauna littering the surface. The stench of rot and decay in the area is foul and almost unbeareable. Bulbous mounds of gore protrude out of sinew-like veins in the earth like a cancer. Beneath the surface, endless tunnels twist and wind towards a deep dank pit in the centre of Boleth's domain. These tunnels, aburst with large carnivorus worms and lined with webs of hair and flesh are no place for the weak stomached.
