The Fortress City of Narasunai is situated atop a cliff-side amongst the [TBD] mountains.  
  The landscape of the City is such that the Noble quarters are situated atop the cliff-face and the wealth of its citizens decreases the further down to the river-side they live. All along the river's banks are a plethora of docks, ports and fishieries vastly ranging in size from small simple docks to massive harbours. Arround the mountain bases, a mix of farms and mines can be found, supplying most of the city's requirements.
