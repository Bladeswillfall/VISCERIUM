## Beliefs

### The seven sins

Those that follow ________ believe that the 7 Nadirs of Sin are created by people, by our own existence and what we do with it. As a result of there being more and more people on [[Lore/Degel System/Errack]], the power of the people has grown and as such, this is why the Nadirs have been showing up and the opening of Rifts is ever more frequent.   Specificaly, _____ teaches that the sins are divided among parts of the body. Those parts are:

- **Greed**: Fingers
- **Gluttony**: Stomach/Intestines
- **Wrath**: Mouth(Teeth?)/Adrenal glands
- **Lust**: Groin
- **Pride**: Heart
- **Envy**: Eyes
- **Sloth**: Body fat/Buttocks
