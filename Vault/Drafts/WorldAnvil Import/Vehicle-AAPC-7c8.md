REF: LVT-4, or 4a. And, ACV-30.  

### Design

Amphibious eight-wheeled design highlighted by a large rear-centre boarding ramp. It is powered by a cutting-edge Hybrid V10 11.7L diesel engine, delivering an impressive 1,305kW. The rear three wheels on each side are shielded by articulating armour, significantly reducing the risk of damage to the tyres. A skirt of heavy chainmail-infused rubber strips, painted to match the surroundings, hangs over the wheel side-profile, enhancing camouflage and concealing the vehicle's armour profile. The front of the vehicle is equipped with a frontal plate, actuated by hydraulic pistons, which can unfurl to form the bow of the hull. With approximately 50cm of standard ground clearance and a v-shaped hull designed for mine resistance, this vehicle excels at navigating even the most challenging off-road terrains.  

### Variants

#### Standard variant

Fully enclosed hull, transports 16 seated troops. Armed with a remote-opperated 15mm HMG/58mm GL turret.

 

#### Droid transport

Fully enclosed hull, capable of charging and transporting 4x Droids and a mule. Armed with a remote-opperated .50BMG GPMG/58mm GL turret.

 

#### Landing craft variant

Open-topped back, two armoured front facing 15mm HMGs, and two side pintle-mounted .50BMG GPMGs. Capable of carying 20 standing troops, with 4 more opperating the turrets.

 

#### Anti-Air variant

(LVT-4/40) Open-topped back, inset armoured 57mm (57x438mm) clip-loaded autocannon turret. Designated as a SPAA.
