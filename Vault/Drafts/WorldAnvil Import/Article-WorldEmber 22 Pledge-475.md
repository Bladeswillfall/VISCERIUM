## Focusing on VISCERIUM

VISCERIUM has, and will continue to be, my focus here on WorldAnvil. I am able to dip in and out of each era dependant on my mood and what interests me at the time. As such, VISCERIUM is able to meet all of my creative needs and I will continue to focus on it throughout WorldEmber '22.  

## Which era(s) will I focus on?

NEARSIGHT and ENTROPY are in desperate need of some direction. I've spent this past month scoping out what I want for these two eras and trying to nail down exactly what their USP will be, alongside many other things. This has all been done privately or off of WorldAnvil for the time being. I'm hoping to change that with WorldEmber '22!  

## Using WorldEmber as motivation

Challenges like [Summer Camp](/w/viscerium/a/summer-camp-pledge--22-article) and my previous attempt at [WorldEmber](/w/viscerium/a/worldember--21-pledge-article) are a great source of motivation for me. I often struggle with motivation and focusing on a single task, WA challenges give me the structre I need to produce some of my best content.
