When violence of action is your core operating principal, you expand upon the capabilities of the LAHMM, and develop this feral machine of warfare, the **HiEnA** - **Hi**gh-mobility **En**hanced **A**rmored vehicle.   Capable of 'silent-running' for ___km through the utilisation of its dual-electronic motors and 85kw range extender in the form of a solid-state battery unit.  

### Design

In its standard configuration, the HiEnA has four doors, a rear boot hatch, and a top-mounted, remote operated turret. Though slower and less agile than the LAHMM, the HiEnA makes up for this with significantly uprated armour and weapons systems. Capable of providing greater defense and offense alike when compared to its smaller brother. In a rather unconventional move, the HiEnA was designed with a V8 5.5L petrol engine and dual-electric motors, as opposed to the more conventional diesel engine. This was done in an effort to reduce the noise signature of the vehicle, increase operator comfort by reducing NVH, provide greater winter capabilities, and to offer a better power-to-weight ratio. This change from the norm alows the HiEnA to opperate in tandem with the LAHMM in most scenarios, only falling short in more extreme terrains, and opperational distance.   Fitted with a reinforced frontal ram bar, the HiEnA is able to push most small to medium-sized vehicles out of its way.   Each seat is fitted with a 4-point harness with quick-release to better ensure opperator safety during roll-over or high speed colision.  

### Armour & Armaments

15mm HMG/Stabilised 23mm chaingun/MLRS/120mm Mortar system. Mine-resistant hull with enhanced ambush protection on account of the increased presence of angled armour.   Multiple chaff smokescreen deploying launchers, one on each flank of the vehicle provide opportunities for obsfucation of the HiEnA and nearby supporting opperators.  

### Power plant

Upon ignition, the HiEnA only utilises the dual-electric motors for locomotion, unless the combustion engine is manually engaged via button-press. Otherwise, once accelerated to 25mph (40kph) the combustion engine automatically kicks in and takes over propulsion of the vehicle. The dual-electric motors can be re-activated during higher speeds to provide a temporary boost of additional acceleration and torque.   In order to aleviate some of the logistical burdens of using petrol over diesel, the V8 5.5L engine utilises cylinder deactivation technology to reduce the number of active cylinders when the vehicle is in "eco" mode, or the acceleration pedal is less than 45% engaged. This mode disables half of the cylinders and can reduce the fuel consumption of the HiEnA by roughly 24%.
