The G12 Devil's Tongue is a 23mm (23x102mm) single-barreled chain-driven autocannon. It has earned its name due to its unusually short barrel, causing the muzzle flash to grow to a significant size and briefly "lick" the muzzle's surroundings in flames, causing no damage but an uncomfortable rise in temperature.
