Rifts are the main orifice through which Myrkild Constructs surface. Though many smaller and more subtle rifts pockmark the landscape, apearing as small cave entrances, sinkholes and burrows, the most prominent ones are twisted and malformed over time to the whims of their respective Nadirs.  
  Rifts are, in the simplest terms, the gateways, entries and exits to the surface, from an unseen network of tunnels and caverns through which the Myrkild move. These gateways, innately radiate corruptive [[Drafts/WorldAnvil Import/Law-Resonance-433]], as does the entire tunnel network due to the presence of so many Myrkild, their proximity and actions in the area warp the landscape around them. This radiating resonance is also what causes Harmonic Stallium to form.
