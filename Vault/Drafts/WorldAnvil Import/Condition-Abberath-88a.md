Once proud [Resonants](/w/viscerium/a/resonance-article) - individuals with heightened sensitivity to the ethereal energies that permeate the world, they succumbed to the allure of greater power, becoming Hollowed. This transformation stripped away their humanity, leaving them vulnerable to the crude sciences of mankind and a final, irreversible metamorphosis into the Abberathi.   The Abberathi are biologically mutated weapons of war crafted by the desperate science of mankind. They are now a tool, a weapon with renewed purpose, reforged from their exotic and magnificent origins into an unthinking creature of destruction.  

## Apperance

Averaging a height between 9.5ft to 10.5ft tall, the Abberathi are lean towers of flesh with elongated arms and unique biological and mechanical features. Features such as their thin skin, now almost transparent, displaying the intricate weave and winding of their dermal papillae layer that once enabled the Resonant to sense the flow of Resonance with great fidelity. Non-biological features like their reinforced skeletal structure - often evidenced by protruding steel plates and joints further remove this beings sense of humanity. And yet the Abberathi weild enhanced strength - despite their lean guise, and an incredible stamina. Their presence is marked with the acrid stench of strange chemicals and charred flesh, creating a noxious miasma that surrounds them, and an unsettling gentle groan with each quietly laboured breath from its enlarged ribcage.   Seldom heavily armoured, Abberathi tend to be adorned in light armour such that they maintain their speed on the battlefield and as such maintain their capability to outmanoeuvre, flank and surprise enemy forces.  

### Adornment

Despite their monstrous nature, Abberathi often become macabre mascots for their military units. Unable to adorn themselves, these living weapons are customised by their comrades in a ritual that blends superstition, camaraderie, and grim humour. This practice, not unlike the tradition of painting aircraft or adorning tanks, transforms the Abberathi into a grotesque standard-bearer - a symbol of unit identity in the nightmarish trenches of this era.   The adornments range from practical to eerily ritualistic. Strips of cloth in regimental colours may flutter from protruding steel joints and gaunt limbs, while salvaged industrial parts are welded to their frame as makeshift armour. Occult charms and pagan talismans, believed to offer protection, might dangle from chains wrapped around their towering forms. Antlers and bones from fallen beasts could be mounted on their shoulders or forearms, creating a primal, intimidating silhouette. Crude, symbolic patterns are often painted onto their flesh with a mixture of industrial chemicals and soldiers' blood, forming runes that reflect faintly in the kiss of sunlight…   These personalised Abberathi serve as rallying points in battle, their distinct appearances allowing soldiers to identify their unit amidst the chaos of war. The practice also serves as a coping mechanism, allowing the troops to project a semblance of humanity onto these once-human weapons, even as they march alongside the very embodiment of war's dehumanising nature.

## Weapons of war

Abberathi serve a crucial role on the battlefield as mobile heavy weapons platforms, akin to anti-tank rifle-bearers but with far greater mobility and firepower. Their enhanced strength and stamina allow them to wield massive, high-calibre weapons that rival the power of small artillery pieces or tank cannons. These weapons, too cumbersome for regular infantry, become deadly instruments in the hands of an Abberath. Their primary function is to engage and neutralise heavily armoured threats that would otherwise be impervious to standard infantry weaponry. Whether facing enemy vehicles or formidable Myrkild constructs, Abberathi are deployed to exploit weaknesses and decimate protective armour with precision and overwhelming force.  

## Ethics/Morals

The creation of these monstrosities weighs heavily on the collective conscience of society. Whispers of the horrific experiments and blasphemous rites required to birth an Abberath spread like a plague through war-torn communities. Many question whether victory is worth such a terrible cost, as the line between protector and abomination blurs with each passing day.   The Abberathi now stand as a grim reminder of the depths to which humanity will sink in its desperation to survive, and the terrible price of power in a world teetering on the brink of oblivion.

## The Vodr

Wardens of the Abberathi, these guardians are folk ceremonially bound to an Abberath in times of war. Trained in the arts of medicine, mechanics and psychology the Vordr serve a crucial role in maintaining the effectiveness and longevity of these living weapons.   In battle, Vordr provide crucial tactical support, guiding their Abberathi through the chaos of war. Their presence on the battlefield has become a common sight, often appearing as a shadowy figure in the wake of their towering charge. The Vordr's role extends beyond mere maintenance, as they act as a lifeline to the Abberathi's fading humanity.   The relationship between Vordr and Abberathi has sparked both fascination and controversy. Tales circulate of unexpected displays of mutual protection, hinting at a deeper connection than mere duty. Some view the Vordr as preservers of the Abberathi's lost humanity, while others see them as grim necessities in managing these living weapons.   As the war progresses, the Vordr have become an integral part of military strategy, their unique skills and bond with the Abberathi proving invaluable in the ongoing conflict.
