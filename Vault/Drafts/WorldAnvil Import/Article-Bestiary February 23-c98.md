## A piece of the trio

Succubi and Incubi are common creatures in fantastical worlds that utilise demons and the sins in their lore. Often they are simply red skined highly attractive humans with horns, wings and a tail.  
I wanted to create something different, as I have tried to do with all of my Demons. And so, I believe I have set the tone for my Succubus and Incubus articles when they come to fruition.   The Concubus is a new addition to the traditional duality of Succubi and Incubi and brings with it a new design with its own quirks and vicious capabilities.
