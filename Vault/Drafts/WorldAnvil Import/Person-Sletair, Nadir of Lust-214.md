An unquenchable thirst for pleasure; desires that go beyond the ideals of just simple moments of ecstasy and an all seeing eye into the hearts and minds of mortals.  
**Sletair**, also known as the **_"Despot of Desire"_** is a being of androgynous apperance with hermaphrodite physicality. Often depicted as having multiple of each sexual organ and with a number of tendril-like apendages.   Sletair is the Nadir of Lust, envigored by the carnal desires, passion and urges of mankind.  
Capable of melting the minds of mortals through sheer ecstasy, or enacting endless torture, Sletair is both celebrated and feared in the minds of man.  

## Personality

Sletair is a monomaniacal being, obsessed with their creed. They work ever tirelessly to add more to their number, to gain power and influence over their kin, for the more who follow Sletair and feel Lust, the more Sletair's power grows.  
Being the Nadir of Lust, Sletair wants to create feelings of desire, lust and ecstasy in their followers. To show them the incredible heights of experience, far beyond any conceivable levels. For those strong feelings serve to further strengthen Sletair's power, these carnal reactions and excitements feed them. With every feeling of pleasure, Sletair too, is pleased.  

## Lust events

Rifts between the planes that have been corrupted by Sletairs power appear as various cloudy white pools of thick viscous liquid scattered throughout slimey limestone rock formations that break though the soil - not unlike hot springs or rock pools. Surrounding the smooth, wet edges of the rocky pools are numerous flowering venus fly traps, erect pitcher plants and various fungi from the Phallaceae family. The area is laden with pinkish-reds, vibrant greens and milky white colours. The pheromones produced by the various flora attract almost everything around whilst acting as a powerful aphrodisiac.

## Worship & Devotion

Throughout time immemorial, organisations, groups and cults have enacted their darkest desires in secrecy. Perhaps unbeknownst to those taking part, this in itself, is regarded as worship of Sletair. For through that hunger to fulfil a desire, to inflict pain, receive pleasure and involve other people in their sin... They engorge Sletair's power.   Those who praise and worship Sletair do so out of disdain for the norms of society. With a hunger for new experiences, new excitement and new highs, the followers of Sletair are bored of society and what it has to offer within the remits of law and social acceptance. So, they look to Sletair to give them new purpose.

## Gifts of Sletair

Through active worship of Sletair, individuals may be granted _boons_, these boons often corrupt the flesh and mind alike, creating unrecognisable beings, representative of Sletair's power. With each gift, the recipient is more and more likely to succumb to the overwhelmingly corrupting force of a Nadir. Yet, knowing the risks these gifts posses, Sletair's followers seek them still. For the new heights they have been chasing are, in their minds, worth the risk.  

## Underlings

Sletair has many followers, some born mortal and then converted, others crafted from Sletair's own essence. Regardless of how they came to be, each serves a purpose. The most renowned of these underlings are the succubi and incubi, infernal creatures tasked with seducing and enticing more souls under Sletairs reign. Just a small taste of the experiences a succubus or incubus can grant are often enough to instantly corrupt the minds of normal folk and leave them ceaselessly craving more.
