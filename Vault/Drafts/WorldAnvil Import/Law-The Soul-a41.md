The soul takes basic emotions and turns them into complex feelings.   Souls are transported along the The Leyline network and are part of what gives the Leylines their magical aura. In additon to this, souls are what permitted the transition from primitive, instinctual thoughts and actions to complex ideas and feelings. Driving the evolution of humanity.
