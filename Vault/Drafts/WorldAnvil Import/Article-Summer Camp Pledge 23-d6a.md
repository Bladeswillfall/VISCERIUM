## Methodology

I aim to achieve Copper by completing prompts across all four tiers instead of focusing on one single tier. I find this allows me to stretch my creativity in ways that are beneficial to VISCERIUM, and prevents me from getting bogged down with prompts that I do not connect with during Summer Camp.

### Nnie's Camp Chill Guidelines

[![Camp Chill Badge for Summer Camp](/uploads/images/523648f96c81cc5d86919917105e28b1.webp "Summer Camp Camp Chill Badge")](/i/4574878 "Summer Camp Camp Chill Badge")

[Camp Chill (ID 4574878)](https://www.worldanvil.com/i/4574878) by [Rin Garnett](https://www.worldanvil.com/author/Those2Nerds)

  
- Remember the prompts are suggestions, not requirements.  
  
- Write what you're passionate about, leave what you aren't.  
  
- Relax when you feel tired.  
  
- Fight off any pesky brain-squitos trying to make you doubt your work.  
  
- Don't abandon your non-summercamp goals! Write that article you're itching to write, even if it doesn't fit any prompts.
