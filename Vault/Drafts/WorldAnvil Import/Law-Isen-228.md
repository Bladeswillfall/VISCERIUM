> Isen Resonants are known for their calm and collected nature, often appearing detached from the world around them. They are analytical and strategic, always thinking several steps ahead of their opponents. Despite their cold exterior, they possess a deep sense of loyalty and duty, making them formidable allies and powerful foes.

  **Isen** is a distinctive form of [[Drafts/WorldAnvil Import/Law-Resonance-433]] that allows its practitioners to manipulate thermal energy at the lower end of the temperature spectrum, able to control cold gas, solids, and liquids alike. Isen Resonance is characterized by its ability to manipulate molecular kinetics, slowing down particle movement to induce various states of matter. Practitioners can control not just ice, but also supercooled liquids, cryogenic gases, and in theory, even exotic states where atoms lose their individual identities and coalesce into a single quantum entity, behaving more like waves than particles.   One of the most fascinating aspects of Isen Resonance is its ability to create thick fogs by drawing down moisture in the air and cooling it. This process showcases the versatility and precision of Isen practitioners.   The somatic component of Isen Resonance is frequently defined by practitioners that utilise flowy movements that end in sharp decisiveness, symbolising the transition of water into ice. Though unnecessary, this distinctive often-adopted style reflects the intricate balance and control required to wield Isen Resonance effectively.  

## Warfare

Isen Resonants excel at countering other forms of Resonance by exploiting the fundamental relationship between temperature and matter states. They can flash-freeze incoming water attacks from Bylga Resonants, turning them into deadly ice projectiles. Against Vindr Resonants, they can condense air currents into liquid droplets or even solid particles. When facing Muld Resonants, Isen practitioners can induce thermal shock in solid objects, causing them to become brittle and shatter.  

Isen Resonants are masters of environmental control. They can create zones of extreme cold that sap enemy strength and willpower. By manipulating dew points and humidity, they can generate thick, ground-hugging fog banks that obscure vision and muffle sound. Their ability to rapidly cool surfaces allows them to create treacherous ice fields or strengthen existing structures by inducing thermal contraction.

The offensive potential of Isen Resonance goes beyond simple ice projectiles. Practitioners can create razor-thin sheets of supercooled air that slice through armour, or launch cryogenic gas clouds that freeze enemies on contact. Defensively, they can form barriers of thermally-resistant materials or create vacuum pockets that insulate against both physical and energy attacks.

  Isen Resonants excel at both offensive and defensive operations. Their ability to cool and crack enemy fortifications makes them effective at breaching defences and creating entry points for allied forces. On the defensive side, they can create impenetrable ice-composite walls to bolster their own fortifications. By inducing thermal stress, they can weaken enemy structures, undermining their stability and integrity.   The precision with which Isen Resonants control temperature makes them indispensable for delicate operations. This includes disabling complex machinery or preserving sensitive materials, showcasing their utility beyond direct combat applications.   Whether creating pathways, breaching walls, or conducting covert sabotage, Isen Resonants are a formidable asset in any siege warfare scenario.

## Utility

Isen Resonants are not only warriors but also invaluable assets in various non-combat roles. They can use their powers to preserve food and medicine, create cold storage facilities, or even aid in the study of cryogenics. Practitioners are crucial in fields such as metallurgy, where they can induce specific crystal structures through controlled cooling. In medicine, they assist with cryotherapy and organ preservation.   While Isen Resonants may not be as specialised as other Resonants in manipulating a single element, their versatility in controlling various states of matter provides them with unparalleled flexibility. This adaptability has made Isen Resonants highly valued in diverse fields, from military strategy to scientific research. Their ability to excel in multiple domains has often placed them in leadership roles or as key advisors in many societies.  

## Cultural and Social Impact

Isen Resonants are a rare occurrence, making up only a small fraction of all Resonants. This scarcity has contributed to a mystique surrounding their abilities, often leading to misconceptions and exaggerated tales of their powers. In many cultures, the birth of an Isen Resonant is seen as a significant omen, sometimes celebrated, other times feared.   The rarity of Isen Resonants, along with their Svar counterparts, has profoundly shaped their place in society and culture. Their unique abilities to manipulate thermal energy and matter states have led to a complex web of social dynamics, cultural beliefs, and societal roles.
