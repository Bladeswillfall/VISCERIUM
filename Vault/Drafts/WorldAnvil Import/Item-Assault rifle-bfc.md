Chambered in .45-70 auto, with a 50rnd magazine and factory-fitted 40mm underslung grenade launcher.  

## UVc rounds

UVc rounds are an advanced form of High Explosive/High Explosive Incendiary (HE/HEI) ammunition designed to combat the Myrkild threat. These rounds feature a unique dual-action mechanism:

- Penetration: At the core of each round is an armour-piercing rod, similar to AP rounds, ensuring deep target penetration.
- UVc Emission: Upon impact, the round's jacket deforms, triggering a compression-based detonation of a specialized UVc putty. This putty, composed of chemical and metallic agents, burns at extreme temperatures, releasing an intense burst of UVc light-emitting radiation.

  UVc rounds were developed to further enhance Humanity's capabilities against the Myrkild threat, no longer requiring such extensive use of incendiaries and flamethrowers on the frontline. These units could instead act as a secondary force following closly behind any offensive actions.
