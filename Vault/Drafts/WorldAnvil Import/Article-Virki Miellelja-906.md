Virki (fort) Miellelja is one of the [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Okse Dominion]]'s most depressing military training camps. Originally, Miellelja was simple just a training camp but over the years, a dedicated fort was constructed to defend the trainees and simultaneously secure the local land.   Located on the western coast, this fort is almost constantly in a flux of cold wind, and icy rain - perfect for hardening recruits and whitteling out the weak.
