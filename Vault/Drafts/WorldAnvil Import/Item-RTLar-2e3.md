## Design Philosophy: Born to Endure

_When survival is the only doctrine, the weapon is the doctrine._   Steel, sweat, and necessity forge the story of the RTL/AR. Purpose-engineered as a squad support weapon, the Rapid-fire Twin Linked Automatic Rifle stands as both weapon and shield - scaled up to confront the monstrous Myrkild and Naranor where survival demands firepower on a new scale.   Optimised for suppressive fire in complex threat environments, the RTL/AR wields the power of .45 CTA cartridges to deliver consistent, overwhelming volume. When conventional arms prove insufficient against swarming adversaries, the RTL/AR delivers the decisive advantage necessary to hold the line.

- **High-Volume Fire**: Alternating barrel system supports extended engagements while minimizing the risk of cook-off or catastrophic failure.
- **Alternating Barrel System**: Each trigger-pull cycles between twin barrels, creating an unbroken chain of fire while one barrel cools and the other continues - a steady, mechanical heartbeat for the front lines.
- **Thermal Management**: Linear fluting maximizes surface area and heat dissipation, while integrated temperature sensors in the barrel assembly support real-time diagnostics.
- **Purpose Over Subtlety**: Designed to eliminate threats and doubts alike, the RTL/AR delivers a concentrated storm of polymer-cased ammunition with disciplined efficiency.

## Key Features: Unforgiving by Design

### Integral Suppressor & Muzzle Brake

- **Suppressor**: The integral suppressor is overbuilt to manage heat soak during rapid strings of fire - rated to withstand upwards of 1,000 rounds before performance drops below hearing-safe thresholds.
- **Muzzle Brake**: Positioned at the end of the suppressor body, a multi-baffle brake system redirects gas, reducing felt recoil by up to 30%, supporting rapid target reacquisition and minimizing signature spillage.

 

### Hot-Swappable Barrel Assembly

No time to wait and no room for failure. The interrupted thread system lets you remove spent barrels and install new ones mid-engagement, maintaining momentum over comfort.

- **Change Protocol**: The interrupted thread lockup allows a trained operator to conduct a barrel swap without tools. A heat-shielded latch and alignment detents make the mechanism manageable even under fire.
- **Component Durability**: Barrels are nitride-treated for corrosion resistance and longevity, easily field-replaceable with serialized tracking for maintenance cycles.

 

### Utility & Weight

Factory bipod and carry handle/foregrip provide stability and portability. Its substantial weight is a trade-off - carried by those who understand its value beyond the typical service rifle.
