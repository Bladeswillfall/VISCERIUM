The city of kites, built on an island in the centre of a river, bisecting it and shaping the island into the form of a kite. A traditional holiday in the city sees it decorated with colourful kites in the skies above as well as streams of coloured cloth and paper strung upon thin ropes. The ropes are strung from building to building, across walls, around fences and they criss-cross every which way.
