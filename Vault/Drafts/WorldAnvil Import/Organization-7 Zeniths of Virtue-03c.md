> Dark are the days, darker still are the nights. Embrace the light and serve the higher purpose. The light purifies all, unclean are those not under it's warmth.
> 
> — Sanctified priestess

  The 7 Zeniths of Virtue, also known as _The Holy Septuplet_, are superior beings of enlightened power, envigoured by the mental state of all sentient beings.   In direct opposition to the Nadirs of Sin, the Zeniths of Virtue desire to cleanse that which is not pure and just. As each Zenith requires the emotions of living beings to engorge their power, they aim to convert more than they destroy, sustaining their power and adding more to their cause.   Sects and congregations have formed across the globe to worship the Zeniths. They work to spread the gospel of the Naranor and convert more to their cause.  

## Purified rifts

When a rift is cleansed by the power of a Zenith, their power flows into the mortal realm alongside the forces of magic, slowly purifying the land with their supernal benediction. This rift is initially not visible, but as the power of the Zenith grows, the appearance of the rift changes too and they are able to bless and purify the area beyond the portal. Once the land becomes saturated, the rift becomes unstable and choirlings can pass through from the aether. They will then prepare the land for the inevitable arrival of their master, constructing strongholds and releasing sanctifiers upon Errack.   Once a Zenith passes through the tear, a powerful detonation of energy occurs and purification spreads at a violent rate. This detonation takes on slightly different appearances based on the Zenith, but the outcome is the same - a great crater formed in the land centred on the rip, and a fiery blast that clears the area of all living life.  

## Cascading Purification

A strange phenomena can be observed in the presence of a Zenith. They seem to be capable of promptly purifying the natural environment around them. All biomass seems to become enlightend into something new, something that is unique to the Zenith and serves a new purpose. This phenomenon is strongest near the Zenith but can be seen far beyond their location, ever flowing outwards into new areas. A similar effect can be seen from the choirlings of a Zenith, although it is on a much smaller scale, only capable of affecting the immediate area over a longer period of time.

### Alisian, Zenith of Humility

The shiest of Zeniths, Alisian, has a very closed posture and tends to keep to themselves, or at least the darker corners of a room. Prefering to not be the centre of attention, Alisian and their underlings make for great spies and counter-inteligence agents.   Alisian is a truly humble being, never one to take credit or be the focus of discussion. As such, it's not uncommon for thier Choirlings or followers to be silenced. Unable to speak, they let their actions speak for themselves as opposed to boasting or undeplaying events.

### Toriel, Zenith of Charity

Toriel is a healer, fixer of the broken and beaten. Not one to sit idle, Toriel and their underlings wonder from place to place, scouring battlefields and remote locations for those in need. Though this aid does not come freely, payment is requested for salvation, your soul. In agreement to serve the Zenith, life may contine on... In a way.   Often compared to a vulture or scavenger, Toriel prefers to view themself as a saviour and restorer.

### Ephemera, Zenith of Temperance

The embodiment of restraint, Ephemera is repulsed by strong flavours, alcohol, drugs and the like. Opting instead to only consume the absolute minimum required to sustain themselves. Often referred to as _"The Monk"_, Ephemera has a very zen and calming personality. This can come across as rather disturbing at times but Ephemera is simply unphased by most situations.   Once moved to action, the Zenith of Temperance moves like a leaf on the wind. With swift agility and true aim, Ephemera gains their nomenclature of _"The Monk"_ from this and their almost perpetual meditaitve state.

### Jurel, Zenith of Chastity

Jurel sees sex, sexuality and carnal thoughts as perverse and sinful. They actively go out of their way to prevent people from sinning by encasing them in iron mechanisms or removing genitalia entirely. Eventually coming to see flesh as a weakeness, Jurel has become an enthusiastic crafter. Creating wonderous machines out of the repentant.   Now dubbed by many as _"The Iron Angel"_, Jurel truly emobies the virtue of chastity through their perpetual need to restrain and remove parts of those that wish to comit carnal acts.

### Mithael, Zenith of Kindness

A being of pure empathy, Mithael has the tendancy to force people into a state not unlike leprosy where they can no longer feel their own pain to the point of accidentally harming themselves, whilst making them slaves to the "greater good" of others. In addition to this, their followers are able to detect even the slightest pain. Constantly overwhelming the mind with negative thoughts and the pain of others that they can't fix.   Mithael is constantly devastated by the pain of others and as such has an almost maniacal drive to remove all pain from reality in it's entirety.

### Sorath, Zenith of Diligence

Sorath is a tireless being, persistent in all that they do. Often dubbed _"The Leader"_, They push and push, encoraging others through their own devotion to a cause. An energetic and relentless Zenith, Sorath is not one to slack or not do something with one hundred percent of their energy.   The Nadir of Diligence's choirlings and followers aim to also showcase this same relentless devotion to that which they do.

### Azelain, Zenith of Patience

_"The Mother"_, Azelain, is a fierce protector of their kin. Potentially the strongest of the Zeniths, Azelain is not to be trifled with. For once they are convinced a cause is just, their actions are swift and final. Though it does take a substantial ammount of effort to envoke a reaction of retribution from the Zenith of Patience, for they are typically understanding and accepting of those who repent.   Azelain is seen as the guardian of the Zeniths, a protector of the Choirlings and a fierce retributionist.
