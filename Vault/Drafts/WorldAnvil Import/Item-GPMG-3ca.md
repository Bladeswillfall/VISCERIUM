Chaingun firing .50 BMG (12.7×99mm) (e.g. GAU-21), integrally suppressed - 3D metal printed barrel.  
Chaingun reduces the ammount of noxious gasses that do not burn-off during firing, increasing sustainability of fire in enclosed/underground settings. In addition, the use of a chaingun prevents the impact of dud-rounds increasing reliability of suppressing fire. Whilst slightly heavier than a non-chaingun weapon, the inclusion of powered exo-suits negates this.
