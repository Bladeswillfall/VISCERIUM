> In the shadowy corridors of power, a child's genetic destiny is auctioned to the highest bidder.

  Corporations have gained unprecedented power over human life, extending their influence to the very genetics of the workforce. The "Corporate Genetic Bidding Program" (CGBP) has become a controversial yet widely accepted practice.  

## The Birth of a New Workforce

As the world is plunged once more into an oppressive era, corporations sought new ways to optimise their workforce. The CGBP emerged as a solution, allowing companies to bid on the rights to genetically modify unborn children to suit their specific needs. This program represents a chilling evolution of corporate influence, creating a new form of indentured servitude that begins before birth.  

## Political Landscape and Adoption

The CGBP found fertile ground within the politically far-right aligned nations of the Aquillan Seas Trade Union (ASTU). While not universally adopted, a significant number of ASTU member states, particularly those closer to Myrkild battlegrounds and frontlines, embraced the program. In these regions, where the threat of Myrkild incursions looms large, authoritarian capitalist systems of government are more easily justified, paving the way for the implementation of such radical programs.   The adoption of the CGBP in these areas is often framed as a necessary measure to ensure human survival and maintain a competitive edge against the ever-present Myrkild threat. Leaders argue that by creating a genetically optimised workforce, they can better defend their territories and maintain economic stability in the face of constant danger.  

## The Bidding Process

The CGBP operates through a series of high-stakes auctions where corporations compete for the genetic rights of future generations. Parents are enticed with substantial financial compensation, which can be used to improve their living conditions or prepare for their child's future. However, this comes at a steep cost - the highest bidder gains the right to alter their child's genetic makeup to suit specific corporate needs.  

### Tailored Genetics

Corporations customise genetic modifications based on their industry requirements:

- Mining corporations engineer short, stocky builds with high stamina and resistance to lung diseases
- Tech companies enhance cognitive abilities and reduce sleep requirements
- Military contractors increase muscle mass, heighten aggression, and boost reflexes
- Space exploration firms focus on radiation resistance and efficient oxygen utilisation

 

## A Divided Society

The implementation of the CGBP has far-reaching consequences, reshaping the very fabric of society:

- **Class Divide**: A new social hierarchy has emerged based on genetic modifications. "Pure" unmodified humans have become a rarity, often viewed as inferior or rebellious.
- **Identity Crisis**: Modified individuals grapple with questions of self-identity and free will. Some embrace their designed purpose, while others rebel against their predetermined roles.
- **Ethical Dilemmas**: Parents face moral quandaries about selling their child's genetic rights, while society at large struggles with the ethics of creating humans for specific corporate purposes.

 

## Military Implications

The CGBP's influence extends beyond civilian life, reshaping the very structure of military forces within ASTU nations. This genetic enhancement program has led to significant changes in squad composition and combat strategies:

### Specialised Squads

ASTU military units now typically consist of a small number of genetically enhanced soldiers working in tandem with advanced robotic systems. These squads leverage the synergy between highly specialised human operatives and cutting-edge AI-driven machines.

- **Genetically Modified Soldiers**: Each squad includes a core of soldiers with genetic enhancements tailored for specific roles such as leadership, combat, or technical operations.
- **Robotic Integration**: Unmanned ground vehicles, aerial drones, and robotic "mules" for logistics support form an integral part of these units.
- **Human-Machine Teaming**: Enhanced soldiers are trained to control and coordinate multiple autonomous platforms simultaneously, maximising operational efficiency.

### Impact on Military Readiness

The CGBP has significantly influenced military preparedness and capabilities:

- **Enhanced Performance**: Genetically modified soldiers exhibit superior physical and cognitive abilities, improving overall combat effectiveness.
- **Specialised Skills**: Genetic modifications allow for the development of personnel with innate aptitudes for critical military roles, from cyber warfare to advanced weapons systems operation.
- **Reduced Manpower Requirements**: The combination of enhanced soldiers and robotic systems allows for smaller, more efficient fighting units.
