> [!authoring]- Need a hand?
>
> **Developing this character**
> Use only the questions that reveal something useful. These are development prompts, not required article headings.
>
> **Start with the person**
> - What do they want badly enough to make poor decisions for it?
> - What do they fear losing, and what are they unwilling to admit about themselves?
> - What makes them immediately recognisable in behaviour, voice, appearance, or reputation?
>
> **Contradiction and pressure**
> - Where does their self-image contradict their actual behaviour?
> - What are they proud of, ashamed of, indebted for, or unable to forgive?
> - Which belief, loyalty, habit, fear, or cognitive blind spot predicts the kind of mistake they make?
>
> **Relationships and leverage**
> - Who can make them behave unlike themselves, and why?
> - What do they control directly, and what do they only influence through office, reputation, money, force, knowledge, Resonance, relationships, or debt?
> - Who benefits from their decisions, and who pays the cost?
>
> **Life that actually shaped them**
> - Which family, class, region, faith, oath, wound, disgrace, opportunity, failure, loss, or success changed their worldview?
> - Which established events materially altered them?
> - What part of their past still creates present behaviour rather than merely biography?
>
> **VISCERIUM lenses**
> - How have their culture, social hierarchy, institution, and era taught them what is normal?
> - If Resonance matters to them, is it capability, stigma, temptation, duty, infrastructure, faith, or threat?
> - Keep the personal stakes legible even when they stand inside much larger wars, institutions, or historical forces.
>
> **Story development**
> - If a story uses them, what pressure could force their want, duty, relationship, and contradiction into the same decision?
> - Keep unrevealed arcs, secret identities, future betrayals, and spoiler consequences in `Vault/Private` unless deliberately public.
>
> **Compose the article**
> - Do not answer these prompts one-by-one.
> - Choose the few ideas that actually distinguish this subject.
> - Group facts by cause, consequence, experience, or tension rather than by questionnaire category.
> - Rename, merge, reorder, or remove the fallback headings when a stronger reader-facing structure emerges.
> - Put history where its consequences are felt; use a dedicated history section only for chronology that still needs one.
> - Use wikilinks as depth valves: link to connected systems, people, places, practices, and events instead of re-explaining them here.
> - Let local voices, notices, prices, jokes, graffiti, complaints, crude detail, and contradictory testimony interrupt polished exposition when they reveal people or institutions; do not polish away the people.
>
> **Go deeper when needed**
> - [[014 - Political Character Authoring SOP]] when institutional power matters
> - [[System/SOPs/Relationship Authoring SOP|Relationship Authoring SOP]]
> - [[Support/Lore Composition SOP|Lore Composition SOP]]
>
> **Before publishing**
> - Remove material that should remain secret.
> - Write a reader-safe `description`.
> - Establish the relevant era or context.
> - Set `published` only when the article first becomes public.
> - Leave optional metadata absent until it improves navigation, presentation, or continuity.
>
> **Boundary**
> This helper is creator guidance, not canon. Do not use it as a secrecy boundary; unrevealed material still belongs in `Vault/Private`.
