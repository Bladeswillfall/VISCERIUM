## Summary

A loose laboratory rule emerged: dangerous Myrkild material should touch as little organic matter as possible.

## Context and Course

The practice developed from repeated failures rather than from a single discovery. Flame-sealed glass, glazed ceramic and metal secondary cases spread unevenly between institutions.

## Consequences and Memory

Later containment engineers retrospectively grouped these local practices under the name “Glass Doctrine.” The phrase does not imply a contemporary international standard.
