## Summary

Attempts to restore the physical wasting of Hollowed patients produced unexpected regenerative and reconstructive responses.

## Context and Course

The work began as treatment, not weapons research. Practitioners were trying to preserve bodies already damaged by Hollowing.

## Consequences and Memory

Much later, military programmes would reinterpret the discovery as proof that Hollowed physiology could support extensive reconstruction.
