## Summary

Preserved Wrath material began moving between private collections, medical rooms and military patrons.

## Context and Course

The trade was irregular and dangerous. Provenance was poor, preservation methods varied, and most buyers had no knowledge of parallel work elsewhere.

## Consequences and Memory

Some laboratories gained repeat access to the same biological material, making comparison possible without creating a unified research community.

## Related

This pin belongs to a local research lineage. Similar work elsewhere may be unknown to its participants and only connected retrospectively by later scholars.
