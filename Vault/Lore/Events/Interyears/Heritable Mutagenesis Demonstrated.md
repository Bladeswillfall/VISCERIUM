## Summary

Comparative family records showed that some altered traits survived migration away from contaminated regions.

## Context and Course

The evidence came from local genealogies, birth records and long-lived exposed communities. It did not yet amount to a modern genetic theory.

## Consequences and Memory

Researchers began separating direct infestation, environmental mutagenesis and inherited change as different biological problems.
