## Summary

Researchers pursuing Wrath-derived enhancement began following two incompatible methods.

## Context and Course

One approach isolated nonliving compounds and tissues. Another deliberately introduced living Myrkild and tried to manage the resulting infestation. Contemporary researchers did not necessarily think of this as a field-wide split; later histories do.

## Consequences and Memory

The derivative branch offered lower performance with greater predictability. The living branch offered exceptional performance while the subject was progressively consumed from within.

This pin belongs to a local research lineage. Similar work elsewhere may be unknown to its participants and only connected retrospectively by later scholars.
