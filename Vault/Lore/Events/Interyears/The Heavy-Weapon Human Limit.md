## Summary

Weapon systems outgrew what ordinary infantry could carry, control and survive using for long periods.

## Context and Course

This engineering problem developed independently from medical work on the Hollowed. The two lines did not need to know of each other yet.

## Consequences and Memory

When military engineering later encountered controlled Hollowed reconstruction, a new solution became imaginable: change the body rather than keep shrinking the weapon.
