## Summary

Researchers found that a Kildhornd did more than weaken Myrkild: it adapted its output to the body carrying it.

## Context and Course

Earlier transplant attempts had produced rejection, uncontrolled growth or host death. The new result explained why an organ taken from one body could not simply be installed in another.

## Consequences and Memory

The problem changed from transplantation to preparation: future researchers would try to prime living tissue for a chosen host before implantation.
