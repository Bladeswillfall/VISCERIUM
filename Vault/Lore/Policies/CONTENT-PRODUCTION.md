This statement explains where VISCERIUM permits AI-assisted tools and where it does not. It applies to material published under the VISCERIUM name.

## Creative content

VISCERIUM has not used, and will not use, generative AI to create published creative content.

All published articles, stories, lore, characters, places, artwork, and other creative material are created by people. Generative outputs are not published as VISCERIUM creative work.

## Technical assistance

VISCERIUM may use AI-assisted tools during technical development. This can include code, site features, troubleshooting, automation, and Obsidian customisation.

These tools are used to support learning and problem-solving. They may explain concepts, compare approaches, suggest technical options, and help identify faults. Technical assistance does not replace human authorship, review, testing, or responsibility.

## Human oversight and accountability

> ### “A computer can never be held accountable, therefore a computer must never make a management decision.”
>
> — *IBM training manual, 1979* ([via IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/ai-decision-making-where-do-businesses-draw-the-line))

VISCERIUM uses a **human-in-the-loop** process.

A person always reviews and tests AI-assisted output. That person decides what VISCERIUM accepts, changes, rejects, publishes, or implements.

AI-assisted tools cannot approve work, claim authorship, or make the final decision. Accountability remains with the person who approves the result.

## Meaning of “human-made”

When VISCERIUM describes content as **human-made**, the term applies to creative and editorial work.

It does not mean that every line of code or every technical process was completed without AI assistance. Technology may help build and maintain the framework; people create the world within it.

## Human Made mark

The third-party [Human Made mark](https://hinokodo.itch.io/human-made) has its own usage conditions. VISCERIUM uses it only where the intended meaning aligns with those conditions and with this statement.

Any Human Made mark or similar label displayed by VISCERIUM links back to this page so the claim can be read in context. Where clearer, VISCERIUM may instead use the wording **Human-created content**.
