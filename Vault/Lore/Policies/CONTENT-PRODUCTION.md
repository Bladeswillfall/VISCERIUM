This statement explains how VISCERIUM distinguishes human-created *creative work* from *AI-assisted technical development*. It applies to material published under the VISCERIUM name and to the technical framework used to publish and maintain it.

## Creative content
VISCERIUM's published lore, fiction, canon, characters, places, artwork, maps, and other creative material are **human-made**.

Generative AI is not used to produce published VISCERIUM creative content. Creative authorship, editorial judgement, and canon authority remain with people.

The presence of technical automation, software assistance, development tooling, or unpublished working material does not change the authorship of the published creative work.

## Technical assistance
VISCERIUM may use AI-assisted tools during technical development and maintenance of the Codex and its supporting creator tools.

This may include generating, modifying, reviewing, or troubleshooting:
  - website code and components;
  - styling and interface implementations;
  - tests;
  - build, migration, and maintenance scripts;
  - configuration;
  - automation;
  - technical documentation;
  - accessibility and performance improvements; and
  - Obsidian customisation and creator tooling.
  
AI-assisted code or other technical output may therefore form part of the software used by VISCERIUM. Such output is reviewed, tested, and accepted by a person before it is implemented.

The use of AI in technical infrastructure does not mean that the lore, fiction, artwork, or other creative material delivered through that infrastructure was generated by AI.

  ### Human oversight and accountability
  > #### “A computer can never be held accountable, therefore a computer must never make a management decision.”
  >
  > — *IBM training manual, 1979* ([via IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/ai-decision-making-where-do-businesses-draw-the-line))
  
  VISCERIUM uses a **human-in-the-loop** process for AI-assisted technical work.
  
  A person reviews and tests AI-assisted output and decides what VISCERIUM accepts, changes, rejects, publishes, or implements.
  
  AI-assisted tools cannot approve work, claim authorship, establish canon, or make the final decision. Accountability remains with the person who approves the result.

## Meaning of “human-made”
When VISCERIUM describes its lore, fiction, artwork, canon, or other creative content as **human-made**, the term refers to the authorship and creation of that creative material.

It does not mean that every line of code, development tool, automated process, or technical component used to build and maintain the Codex was completed without AI assistance.

Technology may help build and maintain the framework; people create the world within it.

## Human Made mark
The third-party [Human Made mark](https://hinokodo.itch.io/human-made) has its own usage conditions.

Where VISCERIUM displays the Human Made mark, it is used to identify the **human-made creative content** described by this statement. It is not intended as a claim that every technical component of the website or its development infrastructure was produced without AI assistance - it wasn't.

The mark links back to this page so that its use can be read in context rather than as an unqualified claim about the entire production stack.
