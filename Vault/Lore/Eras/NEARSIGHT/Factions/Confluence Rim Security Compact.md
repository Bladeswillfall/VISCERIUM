The **Confluence Rim Security Compact**, often referred to as the **CRSC**, the **Compact**, or more colloquially as the **Jade Compact**, is a political, economical and security alliance of nations located around the Confluence Sea. Though founded as a multi-national agreement between many shores, islands, river mouths and coastal powers, the CRSC is heavily influenced by its largest and most powerful member state, whose administrative reach, naval yards, railway standards and civic security doctrines have shaped much of the Compact’s modern identity.

Connecting several continents through sea lanes, railways, river ways, port-cities and island chains, the Compact boasts one of the most integrated transport networks in the world. While the [[Aquillan Seas Trade Union]] maintains the largest naval presence across [[Errack]] and the [[Trans-Continental Socialist Confederation]] holds the most extensive land-based railway system, the CRSC is unmatched in its ability to connect rail, port, river and sea into one continuous system of movement.

Thanks to the Compact’s strict infrastructure laws, shared military standards and vast civil-continuity planning, its members are able to respond swiftly to piracy, famine, [[Myrkild]] breaches, natural disasters and foreign incursions. This has led supporters to call the CRSC the *Shield of the Many Shores*. Its critics, especially those outside the Compact, more often call it the Jade Leash.

## Military

### Chain mobility
The defining strength of the CRSC is not simply its navy or its railways, but its ability to connect one form of movement to another. Where the ASTU dominates long-distance naval projection, and the TCSC dominates continental freight and land movement, the CRSC specialises in the difficult spaces between them.

Ports are designed as railheads. Reservoirs can become seaplane bases. Civilian ferries can be converted into military transports. Island airstrips double as emergency hospitals. Ekranoplan ramps, floodgates, canal locks and coastal rail depots are built into the same operational network.

This doctrine is commonly called **Chain Mobility**. A CRSC force can move troops from an inland factory-city by rail, transfer them directly through a fortified port, cross a strait by ekranoplan or amphibious carrier, land them on an island chain, then resupply them by seaplane, drone barge or river convoy.

This makes the Compact exceptionally difficult to isolate. However, it also means that its strength depends heavily on functioning infrastructure. Destroy the right rail-port, dam, signal tower or harbour gate, and entire sections of the chain can begin to fail.
