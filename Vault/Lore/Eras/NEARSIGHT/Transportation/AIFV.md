REF: Korean K21 IFV.  

### Design

Amphibious, rear central boarding hatch, up-angled extended tracks for greater incline traversal, .50cal coaxial machine gun, 57mm (57x438mm) autocannon capable of 120rpm with 300 rounds of ammunition, twin ATGM missile pod. Hybrid electric and diesel engine. Both the coax and main gun are remote opperated. Able to squat the front end of the vehicle downwards to improve depression angle and create more favourable armour angles against enemy fire. 6x deployable infantry balistic shields, 3x per side - intended for use as stationary cover. Able to transport 8x infantry.  

### Variant 1

57mm (57x438mm) autocannon capable of 120rpm with 300 rounds of ammunition, twin ATGM missile pod.

 

### Variant 2

Armed with a 23mm 850rpm rotary cannon. This variant has 850 rounds ready to fire, and a further 1,300 rounds stowed. Officially, this is a SPAAG variant, however it has also seen frequent use in an anti-light armoured vehicle, and anti-light fortification role.
