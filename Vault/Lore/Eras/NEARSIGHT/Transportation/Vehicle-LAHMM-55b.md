The LAHMM - Light Armoured High Mobility Modular vehicle, also known by it's nickname, the "Lamb". Is the most commonly used military vehicle in service.  

## Design

Inspired by the rugged off-road vehicles utilised by the Reavers, this refined powerhouse of all-terain locomotion would bring speed and lethality to a previously slugish branch of warfare.   With a high ground clearance, low centre-of-mass, and a rugged angular design the LAHMM bares an intentionally aggressive, and imposing, design. A design further showcased by the vehicle's deep, gutteral growl its large diesel engine produces, all the while sporting a military personnel-favourite anti-lag button on the steering wheel for strategic high-speed manoeuvres.  

### Armour & Armaments

[![](/uploads/images/983b7680d23ba2c134c87d394cc9e38f.jpg "rust-shake-5-scene-6.jpg")](/i/2603328 "rust-shake-5-scene-6.jpg")

[335 BRM](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/aRObeR) by [Rust Shake](https://www.artstation.com/ixlrlxi)

Fitted with light-weight medium-level armour all-round, the LAHMM is both defensible and fast. The rear section is left open to allow for a wide-range of equipment or storage sollutions to be fitted here.  
One of the more common armaments of the LAHMM is to mount a powerful 15mm HMG or a 40mm automatic grenade launcher capable of firing up to 400 rounds per minute atop the central turret ring. Alternatively, a lighter .50cal GPMG can also be mounted.

 

### Seating

Due to most people being right-handed, the driver's seat is found on the right-hand side with one passenger seat to the driver's left side. A third person can be transported as the rear gunner within the back section of the vehicle. In this configuration, the centre dividing section is removed and a rear hatch is added to protect the gunner.  
Due to the widespread use of caseless ammo, the passenger is able to use a variety of handheld firearms from within the vehicle to engage targets in combination with the rear gunner.  

### Engine

Utilising a detuned 6.5L diesel flat-six opposed-piston engine from a _____, in combination with a variable twin scroll turbocharger, the LAHMM is capable of rapid deployment in any environment.  
This reliable platform would quickly see widespread adoption across the ____ and as such cause the LAHMM to become a favoured tool for opperators worldwide.  

### Tyres

Instead of opting for airless tyres like some of the heavier military equipment and inner-city transportation vehicles - the LAHMM uses large, thick, off-road, run-flat tyres that are capable of propelling the vehicle effectively through almost any surface type.  

### Equipment

Fitted with an advanced onboard system of sensors, the LAHMM is able to keep the occupants informed of their surroundings at all times, even through heavy weather.   In addition to the sensors, the LAHMM also comes with laser spotlights, blackout lights and toggleable satellite internet.

 

## Variants

The LAHMM comes in a wide range of variants that alter body panels, armour levels, attachments and capabilities. These modifications of the base vehicle enhance its modularity and make it suitable of undertaking a plethora of roles within the armed forces.

### Light utility vehicle

Open cockpit design without doors, exposed frame cage for roll-over protection. Optional canvas canopy can be fitted.  

### Special forces variant

This variant comes with a slightly reduced armoured hull at the rear of the vehicle, removing the vertical panels and retaining only the sloped armour. Additionally the doors are also removed to enhance the mobility of its occupants. A swing-arm mount atop the front wheel-arches just below the corners of the front windshield is added to enable a GPMG to be fired using the vehicle as cover. Additionally, an optional swing-arm mount can be attached to the rear bed of the vehicle for direct rearwards firing.
