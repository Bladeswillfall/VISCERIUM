- Vindr
- Bylga

- Muld
  
  A leader of the council from one of the forms of Resonance is cycled every _ years. The leader cannot have a shadow.  
  A shadow is chosen by the lead member of the representatives. So there will be, for example:- a Svar council leader
- an Isen Leader
- an Isen Shadow
- an Bylga Leader
- an Bylga Shadow
- an Muld Leader
- an Muld Shadow
- an Vindr Leader
- an Vindr Shadow
  
  Shrotly before the Svar council leader's tenure is over, they may find and select a shadow. The shadow in theory, will become the next leader for that form of Resonance in the council.
