Strain pressure: Greed Silhouette: Huge fan-bladed desert or badland growths, black-green with metallic gold seams, glittering from ridgelines like treasure half-buried in the earth. What they really are: Mineral-hoarding succulent lattices with hooked blade-ribs and resin sheaths rich in trace metals. How they compete: Resource capture. They drain sparse moisture, poison surrounding soil with metal-heavy exudate, and snag anything that brushes past them—fur, cloth, carrion scraps, coin purses, tack, banners. Greed’s existing look language—lean, hooked, draconic, gilded—translates perfectly here. Behaviour: Caravan routes slowly drift because teamsters learn to avoid old Fan-fields. Use: Resin for lacquer, lamp oil, false gilding, and luxury incense. Fear: Livestock can get flensed alive in a windstorm if driven through a mature stand.
