Strain pressure: Sloth + general rift wash Silhouette: Vast feather-soft plumes or reeds the height of siege towers, pale grey to ash-white, bending together in slow waves. Logic: Their buried mat absorbs rot and slow seepage from graveyards, plague pits, and abandoned camps. Why they fit: They turn death-sites into visible landmarks without needing to become sentient monster plants. Use: The dried plume-fibre is used in funeral craft, insulation, and ritual masks. Fear: In still air, a Graveplume field can look like a crowd of people standing shoulder to shoulder.
