Strain pressure: Mixed rift corruption, often with stallium-rich ground Silhouette: Ranks of enormous hollow reeds, funnels, and bell-throats rising in clusters around old rifts like a buried cathedral organ breaking the surface. What they really are: Resonance-reactive megaflora with mineralised tissues and stallium-like crystal lamellae. Since rifts radiate corruptive force and that same influence forms harmonic stallium, a plant-like organism that partly crystalises into resonant architecture fits naturally. How they compete: They alter the soundscape and probably pollination itself. Their low tones disrupt animals, fracture fragile growths nearby, and may even affect spore or seed release in surrounding flora. Behaviour: Their notes change before construct movement in the deep. Some Pathfinders use them as warning signs; others call that superstition right up until it saves them. Use: Chime fragments become charms, resonance tools, shrine pieces, and fraud talismans. Fear: Entire villages have relocated because the Organs “started singing again.”
