## Summary

Battlefield physicians described unusual strength, endurance and aggression around some Wrath-strain exposures.

## Context and Course

The reports were practical observations made during war and recovery. They did not form a coherent enhancement programme, and distant physicians may never have seen the same records.

## Consequences and Memory

Later researchers treated surviving case notes as early evidence that Myrkild-driven change could produce useful physical traits.
