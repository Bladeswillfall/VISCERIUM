**Briar Remonnet** was head of the Remonnet family and the last recorded king of Askalia before the country became a republic.

During his reign, the crown appointed members of the Privy Council to advise on military and naval affairs, money, religion, intelligence, law and the royal historical record.

## Assassination

Pressure against Remonnet grew among members of the Senate and the trade guilds. The [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Organisations/The Circle of Trades|Circle of Trades]] participated in a coup in which its members assassinated the king.

The [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Republic of Askalia|Republic of Askalia]] succeeded the monarchy. The Senate became its governing body, while the Circle continued as a private institution with considerable influence over political affairs.

Further details about Remonnet's reign, the circumstances of the assassination and the fate of the royal household remain to be developed.
