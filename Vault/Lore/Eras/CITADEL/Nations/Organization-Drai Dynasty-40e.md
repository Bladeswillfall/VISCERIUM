~~(Due to their lack of Magic users)~~ - The Drai Dynasty are an Authoritarian, Classist, Nation that heavily focuses on an industrialised society. Their core value is "you work to earn a living" (if you're lower class).   The Drai Dynasty are responsible for the discovery of black powder and the subsequent creation of the following:  

 

- Fireworks,
- (TBD missile name) launcher - ref. Hwacha/helstorm rocket battery,
- Hand cannon,
- Tri-barrel hand cannon,
- Grenade,
- Mobile cannon,

And many more.   This is due to their current prowess in persuing the latest and next best seige and warfare equipment. The Drai Dynasty have been stocking up and developing their tools of war for years fueld by their belief that one day they would either be attacked by or would pre-emtively attack magic-wielding Nations.
