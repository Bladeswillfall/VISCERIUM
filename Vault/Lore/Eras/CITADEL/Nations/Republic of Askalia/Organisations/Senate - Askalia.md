The **Senate** is the governing body of the [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Republic of Askalia|Republic of Askalia]]. Its representatives are elected by the local populace of each municipality through an in-person vote.

The Senate existed before the abolition of Askalia's monarchy. Members of the institution exerted pressure against King Briar Remonnet and were involved in the political circumstances surrounding his assassination.

## Government and private influence

Since the establishment of the republic, the Senate has governed separately from the [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Organisations/The Circle of Trades|Circle of Trades]]. The Circle is a private association, but its guilds control many trades and industries on which the nation depends.

Guildmasters use private agreements, payments, blackmail and commercial pressure to influence members of the Senate. Elected office does not necessarily free a senator from dependence upon the guilds that provide employment and wealth within their municipality.

## Electoral limits

The voting population and precise powers of senators have not yet been fully established in the surviving record of the republic. Those questions are especially important to the place of poorer citizens, landless workers and enslaved people in Askalian politics.
