**King's Cliff** is the capital of the [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Republic of Askalia|Republic of Askalia]].

One of the best-established events in the city's working history is the founding of the Porters Guild. Porters in King's Cliff organised to demand better pay, initially forming a workers' union. Their association later spread to other cities and secured a place in the [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Organisations/The Circle of Trades|Circle of Trades]].

The guild's rise is a useful reminder that Askalia's commercial institutions were not all created by wealthy masters. Some were built by people whose work was essential but poorly paid.

The city's districts, defences, population and physical layout have yet to be established.
