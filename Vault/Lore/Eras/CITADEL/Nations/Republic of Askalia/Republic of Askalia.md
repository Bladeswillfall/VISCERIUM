*The golden nation of Errack, where anything can be bought for the right price.*

The **Republic of Askalia** is among the wealthiest trading nations in Errack. Its merchants deal in goods from across the continent, its guilds control much of the country's industry, and fortunes change hands in quantities that would seem absurd to the common folk of less prosperous nations.

Money moves quickly here. Whether it stays in one's possession is another matter.

Askalia is a predominantly temperate country, with broad grasslands, rolling hills and scattered woodland. Much of the land is relatively flat, allowing roads and settlements to extend across great distances without the natural obstacles found elsewhere in Errack. The soil varies in quality, though the warmer regions support vineyards, fruit orchards and other valuable crops.

The republic has earned its reputation for enterprise. Skilled tradespeople can build successful businesses, merchants have access to extensive markets, and the guilds offer opportunities for training and employment.

There are fortunes to be made in Askalia. There are also people who will spend their entire lives working to make them for somebody else.

> "There's no shortage of work in King's Cliff. The trick is getting paid for it."
>
> — A porter's complaint

## History

### The Kingdom of Askalia

Before the republic, there was the Kingdom of Askalia, ruled in its final years by [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Characters/Briar Remonnet|King Briar Remonnet]].

The monarchy governed through the crown and its appointed advisers. The king's Privy Council included authorities responsible for the army, navy, treasury, religion, intelligence, law and royal history. Members were chosen personally by the reigning monarch, placing considerable responsibility in the hands of individuals who owed their positions to royal favour.

The kingdom also had a [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Organisations/Senate - Askalia|Senate]] and an established body of trade guilds. Both had accumulated influence, and both became increasingly hostile to the king's rule.

The [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Organisations/The Circle of Trades|Circle of Trades]], whose Guildmasters represented some of Askalia's most powerful industries, eventually took part in a coup against Remonnet. Under pressure from members of the Senate and the guilds, members of the Circle assassinated the king.

His death brought an end to the Kingdom of Askalia.

### The Republic

The Senate became the governing body of the new republic, with the Circle of Trades continuing as a private organisation independent of the government.

The separation did little to diminish the Circle's influence.

The guilds retained their wealth, their members and their control over the industries on which Askalia depended. They remained capable of influencing senators through private agreements, financial inducements, blackmail and other means.

It was possible to remove the king without removing the interests that had prospered during his reign. Many of those interests had helped kill him.

The precise fate of the old Privy Council and its responsibilities following the coup remains less clear. Askalia's transition from monarchy to republic did, however, leave the Senate governing a country whose commercial institutions were already firmly established.

## Government and politics

### The Senate

The [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Organisations/Senate - Askalia|Senate]] governs the Republic of Askalia. Its members are elected by the local populace of each municipality through in-person voting.

Representation gives municipalities a place in national affairs, although the precise qualifications for voting and the extent of each senator's authority have yet to be established.

The Senate must also contend with the influence of the Circle of Trades.

Guilds hold considerable power over employment, production, commerce and the movement of goods. Their Guildmasters have interests in legislation and public appointments, and the wealth to pursue those interests. A senator may answer to the people who elected them, but may also find themselves dependent upon the merchants and guilds whose business sustains their municipality.

Private meetings between senators and Guildmasters are commonplace within Askalian politics. Some concern legitimate matters of trade and industry. Others involve payments, threats or agreements which neither party would care to acknowledge publicly.

### The Circle of Trades

The [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Organisations/The Circle of Trades|Circle of Trades]] is an association of Askalia's most prominent guilds. Its members have spent generations establishing standards of workmanship, collecting specialist knowledge, organising labour and protecting their respective industries.

The Circle's origins lie with the first Guildmasters to bring their trades under organised leadership.

Balthasar Rolland of the Banking Guild, Fainnear Morgan of the Monster Hunting Guild, Iomhair Grayriver of the Forgemasters Guild, Liliana Dusksong of the Carpenters Guild, Rania Liafiel of the Stonemasons Guild and Talan Umber of the Farmers Guild are named among its founders.

The Circle has since grown to encompass twenty-one guilds, divided between four ranks.

#### The Platinum Eye

The oldest and most influential guilds occupy the Platinum Eye: Banking, Monster Hunting, Forgemasters, Carpenters, Stonemasons and Farmers.

Unlike the Guildmasters of the lower circles, members of the Platinum Eye hold legacy seats. Each is selected by their predecessor rather than elected by the guild's membership, preserving a line of succession established by the founding Guildmasters.

The Platinum Eye traditionally falls under the authority of a Grand Overseer, a position appointed by the nation's ruler to maintain cooperation and loyalty among the guilds. No new Grand Overseer has been elected since the assassination of Briar Remonnet.

#### The Golden Circle

The Golden Circle comprises the Alchemists, Tailors, Guardsmen and Shipwrights guilds.

These trades have considerable importance to Askalian society. Alchemists provide remedies and other concoctions, while the Tailors Guild maintains standards across the production of clothing, from everyday garments to the elaborate work commissioned by wealthy patrons.

The Guardsmans Guild has an especially influential position. It regulates much of the professional protection work undertaken in Askalia, including employment as guards, bodyguards and peacekeepers.

Shipwrights organise labour and materials for the construction of vessels. Their guild originally developed as a means of coordinating large orders from nobles whose ambitions at sea exceeded the capabilities of individual workshops.

#### The Silver Circle

The Glassworkers, Tanners, Hunters, Miners and Fletchers guilds make up the Silver Circle.

Their work ranges from the manufacture of everyday goods to occupations carrying considerable personal danger.

The Tanners Guild, for example, arose partly from the difficulties of processing unfamiliar creatures brought back by hunters and Pathfinders. The knowledge gathered by its members reduced wasted materials and improved the handling of unusual hides.

The Hunters Guild shares information about dangerous territories and sightings of hostile creatures. Its members must be particularly careful when travelling beyond settled lands, where the threat of Demons can turn an ordinary hunt into a fatal undertaking.

The Miners Guild, initially formed to consolidate the interests of competing mining organisations, has also improved wages and safety for its members.

#### The Copper Circle

The Copper Circle consists of the Brewers, Whalers, Fishermen, Pathfinders, Historykeepers and Porters guilds.

The Brewers Guild has helped reduce deaths caused by poorly produced alcohol, while the Fishermans Guild organises fishing routes and regulates access to particular waters.

The Pathfinders Guild gathers maps and accounts from its members, reducing unnecessary journeys and improving knowledge of distant regions. The Historykeepers preserve written histories, records and tapestries within an extensive bibliotheca.

The Porters Guild began with workers in [[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Kings Cliff|King's Cliff]] demanding better pay for their labour. It grew from a local workers' union into a recognised guild with representation in the Circle of Trades.

Despite their different origins, each guild has an interest in protecting its members and maintaining a place within Askalia's commercial system. That interest does not always extend to people outside the guild.

## People and society

Askalians are often regarded as enterprising, ambitious people. Commerce is part of everyday life, and the republic has produced some of the wealthiest individuals in Errack.

Its poorest inhabitants live in circumstances scarcely comparable to those of its wealthiest citizens.

Guild membership offers one means of improving one's position. An apprenticeship can provide a person with a recognised trade, access to experienced craftspeople and the prospect of earning a respectable living. For those able to establish businesses of their own, the republic's markets offer substantial rewards.

Such opportunities depend upon circumstances.

A young craftsperson from a wealthy family may have ready access to materials, training and a workshop in which to begin their trade. Someone without those advantages must find other means of entering the profession, often relying upon an established employer or the acceptance of a guild.

The guilds themselves vary. Some have developed from associations of successful merchants and craftsmen, concerned chiefly with protecting their businesses. Others began with workers attempting to secure fair wages or improve dangerous working conditions.

Both kinds of organisation now sit within the Circle of Trades, although their interests do not always coincide.

### Wealth and poverty

Wealth affords considerable freedom in Askalia. Those who possess it can purchase land, commission goods, employ protection and pursue opportunities unavailable to most of the population.

Those without sufficient coin must concern themselves with earning it.

The poorest Askalians may find themselves dependent upon employers and guilds whose conditions they have little power to negotiate. In a country where so much work is organised through recognised trades, exclusion from the relevant guild can make an occupation difficult or impossible to pursue.

Yet the republic's prosperity is not entirely reserved for inherited wealth. Its markets provide opportunities for skilled and determined individuals to rise beyond the circumstances of their birth, even if such successes do little to improve the position of everyone left behind.

### Slavery

Slavery is an established part of Askalia's economy.

The republic is particularly known for the sale of enslaved people trained to perform specialised work. Their occupations, abilities and experience affect the prices they command, with those possessing valuable skills often sold for considerably greater sums.

Training an enslaved person for a particular trade can therefore be profitable.

Their labour may be useful in a workshop, household or other occupation, but their expertise affords them no inherent freedom. In the markets where they are sold, their skills are assessed alongside the price of their ownership.

The practice is widespread enough to constitute one of Askalia's recognised exports, even within a nation whose wealth depends so heavily upon the abilities of its working population.

> "A Guildmaster can tell you exactly what a pair of hands is worth. Ask the owner of those hands and you may hear something else."
>
> — Anonymous Askalian observation

## Trade and industry

Askalia's economy is built around commerce, agriculture and specialised industry. Its relatively accessible landscape supports the movement of goods between settlements, while the guilds provide much of the organisation required to produce, transport and sell them.

Bankers safeguard deposits and maintain accounts. Craftspeople produce goods for local use and export. Shipwrights construct vessels for wealthy customers, and the Porters Guild provides labour essential to moving cargo.

The arrangement has allowed Askalia to become a major centre of trade within Errack.

### Wine

Wine is among the republic's established exports.

The country's warmer regions are suited to growing the berries used in its production, and vineyards supply a substantial trade. Wine is common at celebrations and social occasions, particularly among wealthier Askalians.

The Brewers Guild has also encouraged improvements in production, reducing the dangers associated with badly prepared alcohol and allowing successful producers to experiment with more expensive varieties.

### Citrus fruits

Lemons, oranges and grapefruits grow in abundance across suitable parts of Askalia.

These crops are traded widely, adding to the produce carried by the republic's merchants and providing income for the farmers and traders involved in their cultivation and sale.

### Enslaved people

Askalia's trade in enslaved people includes individuals trained for particular occupations.

A purchaser may pay considerably more for a person with useful experience or specialised knowledge than for someone without such training. Different skills command different prices, according to their demand.

It is an established and profitable trade, sustained through the buying and selling of people whose labour and expertise are treated as commodities.

## Defence and security

Askalia depends upon several organisations to maintain security within its settlements and beyond them.

The Guardsmans Guild is responsible for much of the professional protection work undertaken throughout the republic. It was originally formed to regulate guards and other security personnel, with membership becoming a requirement for those wishing to earn a living in such occupations.

The arrangement gives the guild considerable authority over who may work as a gatekeeper, bodyguard or peacekeeper.

Dangerous creatures present a different problem.

The Monster Hunting Guild was founded by guardsmen, hunters and soldiers who sought to organise the capture, study, killing or relocation of dangerous beasts and infected creatures.

It is also responsible for allocating Juggernauts to important locations and ensuring that they receive appropriate equipment and training.

Hunters and Pathfinders contribute further knowledge of the territories beyond Askalia's settlements. The Hunters Guild records sightings of dangerous creatures and shares information intended to prevent needless deaths. The Pathfinders Guild collects maps and travel accounts from those exploring unfamiliar lands.

Their work is valuable because the routes beyond well-settled areas remain dangerous, regardless of the wealth of the nation from which a traveller departs.

## Places of note

### King's Cliff

[[Lore/Eras/CITADEL/Nations/Republic of Askalia/Kings Cliff|King's Cliff]] is the capital of the Republic of Askalia.

The city has a place in the nation's commercial history as the birthplace of the Porters Guild, which began as an association of workers seeking better wages. From those beginnings, the guild spread into other Askalian settlements and eventually became part of the Circle of Trades.

### Saffronport

Saffronport is one of Askalia's recognised cities.

Its history, industries and relationship with the wider republic remain to be developed.

## Wildlife

The grasslands, forests and coastal waters of Askalia support a wide variety of animals.

Foxes, lynx and monkeys inhabit the land, while its birdlife includes golden eagles, black-shouldered kites and the distinctively crowned hoopoe.

The seas are home to sharks, turtles and giant devil rays, alongside the other creatures pursued by Askalia's fishers and whalers.

For those whose livelihoods take them beyond settled areas, familiarity with the local wildlife can be as important as knowledge of the roads. The Hunters and Pathfinders guilds collect and share such information, although there remain territories in which even experienced travellers are poorly prepared for what they may encounter.

## Storyteller View

### Recognisable presence

Guild seals, market stalls, workshops, cargo handlers and a near-constant concern with accounts and payment distinguish Askalian commerce. A merchant can cross from prosperous trading streets to hard-pressed labouring districts without leaving the same city.

### Current agenda

The Senate governs while the Circle of Trades seeks to retain its commercial influence. Guildmasters have reasons to protect existing rights and privileges, even when individual guilds disagree over wages, safety and who should be allowed to work.

### Preferred methods

Private bargaining, patronage, economic pressure and the influence of guild membership shape much of Askalian public life. The Guardsmans Guild regulates professional protection; the Monster Hunting Guild manages specialised threats.

### Resources and limits

Askalia has wealthy merchants, established commercial networks, specialised crafts, trained labour and guilds capable of organising projects beyond the means of individual workshops. Access to money, a recognised profession or influential contacts remains uneven.

### Internal tensions

Municipal representation sits uneasily beside private commercial authority. Some guilds protect workers, while others restrict entry into lucrative occupations. The republic's opportunities coexist with a trade in enslaved people.

### Consequences of involvement

An outsider may find employment, trade or patronage in Askalia, but must deal with guild rules, competing interests and the cost of doing business. Travellers still face dangerous roads and hostile creatures beyond settled territory.
