## Geography

Situated in a dense forest, just beyond the coastline of a craggy cove, the 'Hidden city' is a minor settlement within the Drai Dynasty's vassal state of ____. Flanked by toweirng cliffs wrapped in dense forest and greenery, the cove is very secluded and seldom used by large vessels due to the dangerous nature of entering the shallow waters during rough weather, for all the towering spires .   Sumgyeojin-si itself is nestled in the valley of a steep mountain range, an often calm river runs through its core and flows into the cove.
