The Krassian Wolves are a specialised group of raiders emboldened by Krassian witchcraft. Their boats terrorise the shores in groups of 4 or more after their namesake. Thus fulfilling the legend of their name, the Wolfpack.   The Krassian Wolves are one of the [[Archived - Okse Dominion]]'s main suppliers of Thralls and Ambátts.
