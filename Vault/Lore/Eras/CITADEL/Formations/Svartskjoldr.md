Defenders of oil infrastructure; industrial weaponry and coordination with Resonants.   Given oil’s critical role in the Dominion’s economy and warfare, the Svartskjoldr (Black Shield(s)) are specialised troops tasked with protecting oil fields, refineries, and convoys.  

### Role & Tactics

- Defensive operations against raiders, saboteurs, and Myrkild outbreaks in industrial zones.
- Use of oil-based weaponry (flamethrowers, slick traps)

  The Svartskjoldr. symbolise the Dominion’s industrial heart and the lengths it will go to protect its lifeblood
