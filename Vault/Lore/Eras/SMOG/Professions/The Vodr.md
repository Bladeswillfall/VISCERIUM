Wardens of the Abberathi, these guardians are folk ceremonially bound to an Abberath in times of war. Trained in the arts of medicine, mechanics and psychology, the Vordr serve a crucial role in maintaining the effectiveness and longevity of these living weapons.  

## Origins and Purpose

The creation of the Vordr corps arose from necessity as the Abberathi program expanded. Military strategists quickly realised that these towering, mutated soldiers required specialised care to maintain their combat readiness. Thus, the Vordr were born - part caretaker, part handler, and wholly dedicated to their charges.  

## Training and Selection

Vordr undergo rigorous training that combines elements of combat medicine, weapons expertise, and psychological conditioning. The selection process favors individuals with strong emotional resilience and tactical acumen, traits essential for working with the emotionally detached Abberathi. In the shadowed halls of military academies, aspiring Vordr learn to navigate the fine line between compassion and detachment. They must care for their charges without succumbing to the horror of what the Abberathi have become.  

## Responsibilities

The duties of a Vordr extend far beyond simple maintenance:

- **Physical Care**: Tending to wounds, cleaning the Abberathi's massive frame, and ensuring proper functioning of their augmentations. Many Vordr bear scars from this intimate work, silent testaments to the dangers of their profession.
- **Equipment Management**: Maintaining and preparing the Abberathi's formidable arsenal. The clang of tools and the acrid smell of weapon oil are constant companions to the Vordr.
- **Tactical Support**: Assisting in battlefield positioning and target acquisition. In the chaos of battle, the Vordr's voice often serves as an anchor for their Abberathi.
- **Psychological Monitoring**: Observing the Abberathi for signs of further degradation or unexpected behavior. This task often weighs heaviest on the Vordr, as they witness the slow erosion of what remains of their charge's humanity.

 

## The Bond

The relationship between an Abberathi and its Vordr is formalised through a solemn ritual. A distinctive runic sigil is tattooed on the middle finger of both the Abberathi and the Vordr, serving as a symbol of their bond and a means of identification in the fog of war. This mark, often incorporating elements representing their unit and personal history, becomes a focal point of their shared identity. In quiet moments between battles, some Vordr have been observed tracing the outline of their sigil, a gesture mirrored by their Abberathi in a rare display of connection.  

## Cultural Impact

The Abberathi-Vordr relationship has become a subject of fascination and controversy in wider society. Some view it as a noble calling, preserving a fragment of the Abberathi's lost humanity. Others see it as a grim necessity, managing dangerous living weapons. Whispered tales speak of Vordr who have gone to extraordinary lengths to protect their charges, and of Abberathi displaying unexpected moments of lucidity or protection towards their Vordr. These stories, whether true or embellished, serve to humanize the otherwise monstrous image of the Abberathi. As the war grinds on, the sight of a towering Abberathi followed closely by their dedicated Vordr has become a common, if unsettling, image on the battlefield. For most… It stands as a reminder to the lengths humanity will go in its struggle for survival, and the complex bonds forged in the crucible of war.
