## Summary

Abberathi moved from experimental subjects into battlefield use.

## Context and Course

Mechanical maintenance alone proved inadequate. Carers with medical, technical and behavioural knowledge became essential to keeping each weapon effective.

## Consequences and Memory

The Vodr emerged from this practical need. Reports of recognition, attachment and mutual protection also complicated the assumption that reconstructed Hollowed were entirely empty.
