## Summary

Living Myrkild produced dramatic physical gains while continuing their normal consumption of organic matter.

## Context and Course

Researchers did not solve infestation. They only began measuring how long a subject remained useful before internal consumption became catastrophic.

## Consequences and Memory

Operational value became inseparable from a countdown: the research problem shifted toward delaying consumption rather than eliminating it.
