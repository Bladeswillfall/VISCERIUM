## Summary

Researchers learned to rebuild Hollowed physiology with enough repeatability to plan changes rather than merely react to them.

## Context and Course

Medical reconstruction still existed independently from heavy-weapon engineering until institutions with military interests brought the two problems together.

## Consequences and Memory

The technique supplied the biological half of what would become the Abberath programme.
