## Summary

Researchers found a way to slow internal consumption without stopping it.

## Context and Course

The suppressant did not cure infestation. Subject survival remained unpredictable, and treatment failure could be abrupt.

## Consequences and Memory

The apparent operational window encouraged military trials that would otherwise have been impossible to justify.
