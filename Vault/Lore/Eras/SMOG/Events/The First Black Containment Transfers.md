## Summary

Dangerous biological work was transferred away from ordinary laboratories into facilities designed for secrecy as much as safety.

## Context and Course

Public reforms tightened containment. In parallel, classified programmes used the same lessons to build sites capable of sustaining research that could no longer survive public scrutiny.

## Consequences and Memory

The catastrophe accelerated both safer laboratory practice and the emergence of “black containment” as a separate institutional culture.
