Researchers prepared Kildhornd tissue for a chosen host before implantation and achieved bonds lasting beyond immediate surgical recovery.

## Context and Course

These trials were not a single international programme. Different institutions pursued therapeutic, enhancement, military and control goals independently.

## Consequences and Memory

Artificial symbiosis became a practical research problem rather than an anatomical curiosity.
