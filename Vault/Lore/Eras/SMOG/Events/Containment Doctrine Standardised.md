## Summary

Some SMOG laboratories combined older quarantine customs with industrial ventilation, sealed surfaces, incineration and specialist handling teams.

## Context and Course

Standards remained regional and institutional. Similar facilities could use different terminology, equipment and thresholds.

## Consequences and Memory

The new procedures made more dangerous research possible while also creating confidence that containment could be engineered.
