## Summary

Researchers began using exposure, breeding records and tissue comparison to pursue chosen traits rather than merely document damage.

## Context and Course

This work grew from several local traditions and research problems. It was not yet one coordinated genetic science.

## Consequences and Memory

The field supplied conceptual groundwork for later state and corporate genetic programmes without making those later systems inevitable.
