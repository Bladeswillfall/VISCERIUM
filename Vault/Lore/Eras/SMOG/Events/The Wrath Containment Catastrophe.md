## Summary

Multiple living-strain subjects deteriorated during an experimental extension of their suppression window.

## Context and Course

The catastrophe was not a simple broken-door accident. Active infestations, human subjects, secrecy, transport, underreported failures and institutional pressure combined into a cascading containment loss.

## Consequences and Memory

The surrounding population suffered extensive casualties. Publicly, the disaster discredited the programme. Privately, surviving data revealed why some subjects lasted longer than others.
