## Summary

A minority of experimental bonds survived long enough to become persistent biological states.

## Context and Course

Results varied by programme and purpose. Therapeutic work, enhancement, military hybrids and command subjects could all produce different forms of partial stability.

## Consequences and Memory

The existence of long-lived hybrids made the boundary between treatment, enhancement and weapon design increasingly contested.
