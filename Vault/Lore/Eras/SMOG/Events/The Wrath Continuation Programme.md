## Summary

The programme continued under tighter secrecy after public termination.

## Context and Course

Catastrophe data allowed researchers to compare why some subjects survived longer than others. The underlying Myrkild consumption remained unsolved.

## Consequences and Memory

Derivative treatments widened into more ordinary military use while living-strain work became rarer, more secretive and more dangerous.
