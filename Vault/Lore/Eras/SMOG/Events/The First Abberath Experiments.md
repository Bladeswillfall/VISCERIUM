## Summary

Two previously separate problems met: bodies that could be extensively reconstructed, and weapons ordinary bodies could no longer support.

## Context and Course

The early work was experimental and institution-specific. It should not imply that all Hollowed research or heavy-weapons programmes shared the same aims.

## Consequences and Memory

The Abberath concept emerged: a reconstructed former Resonant built around strength, endurance and weapon carriage rather than recovery of ordinary human form.
