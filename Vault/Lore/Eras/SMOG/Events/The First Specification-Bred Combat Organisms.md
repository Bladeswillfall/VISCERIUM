## Summary

A small number of military programmes moved from useful altered animals to organisms developed for a specified combat role.

## Context and Course

Earlier precedents such as Vihakult husbandry and Verazemian horticulture were local practices, not steps in a shared programme. Later historians connected them retrospectively.

## Consequences and Memory

Assault, tracking, breaching, contaminated-terrain transport and anti-Myrkild hunting became plausible design briefs for purpose-bred organisms.
