So, I figure, with demons and big guns etc, identifying bodies isn't gonna be the easiest, right? And, when you're at war, if you get news someone has died, you might need time to process that until your effective again? following? Sooooooo... My idea is that they get tattooed on their chest or something like that, their name, DOB, Where they're from, next of kin etc... And if someone on their list of people to contact dies, they scratch it out, by slicing it. They then get sent back to the barracks or whatever and are barred from missions until after the cut heals.
