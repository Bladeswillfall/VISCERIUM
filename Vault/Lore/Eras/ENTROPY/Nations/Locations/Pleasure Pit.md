One of the largest and most popular nightclubs in Kisara - the Pleasure Pit is located underground inside of what was once an old munitions warehouse built and utilised during _____. It's tall arched ceilings and tiered floors make great use of the dark space. With bare stone walls still displaying the scars of excavation, black metal pipes, railings and fixings running throughout and smooth hard floors in most areas, the club's industrial theme is almost paletable.   Lit with prodominently red, pink and purple hues, the atmosphere here when in use is electrifying with bloodrushing adrenaline, lust and euphoria. The giant speakers ensure that punchy bass rattles through the chest of all who enter. The lighting is almost trance-enducing, smoke machines, lasers, holo-projections and spotlights all programmed seamlessly by a rudimentary AI to sync with the energising music in the most exhilerating way.   To say the Pleasure Pit is _infamous_ would be an understatement. Almost everyone living in the under-belly of Kisara know of the Pleasure Pit - not all know where to find it, but they've certainly heard of it.  
  It is well known for hosting some of the largest and most talked about raves of the century. As well as being rumoured that they are able to provide access to an equally dangerous and life changing service for those who know what to ask for in the back rooms.  

## Club layout

### Entrance

Just off of the busy city street down a dark dead-end alley way between buildings, two large flat-black doors stand indiscriminately on one side, lit only by the nearby colourful lights, signs and reflections of Kisara. No markings adorne the door nor it's surroundings.   Upon opening the doors, a short dimmly-lit corridor terminates at a large cargo elevator. The elevator only has two buttons, up and down. Down being the only option that is lit by a pinkish-red glow.  

### Lobby

As the wide elevator doors open, the subtle rumble of music can be both heard and felt.

### VIP Floor

Multiple small stages and dance poles are strategically placed throughout the area, allowing guests to indulge in new experiences whilst seated and being waited on.  
The soundproofing provided by the thick stone walls and soft coverings ensure that the music from the main club area doesn't disturb the VIP guests, creating a truly exclusive and private experience.  

#### Private rooms

Within the VIP area of the Pleasure Pit, a series of rooms line the walls on either side. Some with one-way windows that offer a voyeuristic view and others completely sound proofed, ensuring total privacy. Each room is adorned with plush furnishings, ranging from comfortable sofas and armchairs to lavish beds and benches. All of this is accsesible via a softly lit corridor that leads out of the main VIP area and then loops around the back side of the rooms connecting them.   To access the VIP rooms, you must first pass the stationed guardsmen and then use your unique pass card to unlock one of the pre-selected room doors.
