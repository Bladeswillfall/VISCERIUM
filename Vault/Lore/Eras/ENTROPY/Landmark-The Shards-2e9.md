Errack's asteroid belt is accentuated by large, sharp, crystaline-shaped rocks that make up the visage of a thorned crown around Errack's waist. These shards, orbital station-fortresses tethered to Errack are stations carved into, and reaching outwards, along one side of large asteroids that have been fetched from the far reaches of the Degel system, now bound to Errack by space elevator.   The use of such large, dense asteroids serves a dual purpose. Namely to act as a shield for the station-fortress and its tether by providing protection from Errack's asteroid belt. The secondary purpose is to provide a foundation upon which all manner of development can occur.   Looming in Errack's orbit, the eight Shards stand as near-impregnable bastions of survival, their silhouettes a stark reminder of humanity's tenacity in the face of cosmic hostility.  

## Overview

The Shards consist of four major and four minor structures, each a unique blend of advanced technology and desperate survival. These massive constructs serve as both shields against the dangers of Errack's asteroid belt and as havens for those seeking escape from the planet's surface.  

### Maintaining orbit

To preserve the delicate balance of a geo-stationary orbit, massive thrusters have been intricately carved and recessed into the dense asteroids. Protected by thick shielding domes, these thrusters fire infrequently, making precise adjustments to the fortress' orbit and ensuring each station's stability with relative precision.  

### Colision mitigation

While the asteroid itself serves as a primary shield, additional defensive measures ensure the longevity of the fortress. An intricate network of active debris tracking and deflection systems blankets the structure. This includes a vast array of quantum sensors, high-energy plasma turrets, smart missiles, and advanced magnetic field manipulators. At the heart of this defense grid, a predictive AI constantly analyses orbital patterns, coordinating the defenses to neutralise threats before they materialise, turning the space around the fortress into a near impenetrable aegis against the void's dangers.

### Fortress Architecture

Each Shard is a unique marvel of brutal, utilitarian design. Their exteriors are a maze of reinforced bulkheads, armoured plating, and retractable weapon systems. Massive gun emplacements dot the surface, capable of unleashing devastating barrages against both interstellar threats and rogue asteroids. Key architectural features include:

- Layered titanium-alloy hull plating, designed to withstand impacts from micro-meteors and energy weapons
- Modular compartments that can be sealed off in case of hull breaches or Myrkild incursions
- Retractable shield generators that create an energy barrier around critical areas
- Maze-like interior layouts with numerous chokepoints and defensible positions
- Automated defence turrets in corridors and public spaces, ready to activate at a moment's notice

 

#### Defensive Capabilities

The Shards' defensive systems are a testament to paranoia-driven engineering:

- Colossal rail guns capable of accelerating projectiles to a fraction of light speed, effective against both asteroids and hostile spacecraft
- Point-defence laser arrays for intercepting smaller threats and boarding craft
- Electromagnetic pulse generators to disable enemy electronics
- Holographic decoy projectors to confuse incoming attacks
- Dedicated hangar bays for fighter squadrons and interceptors

#### Interior Fortifications

Within the Shards, life is a constant reminder of the dangers that lurk both in space and potentially within. The interior design prioritises security and rapid response:

- Blast doors separate each major section, capable of withstanding both explosive decompression and Myrkild assaults
- Panic rooms are scattered throughout, offering temporary refuge for civilians during emergencies
- Armouries are strategically placed, allowing for quick distribution of weapons to security forces and trained civilians
- Advanced AI-driven surveillance systems monitor for any signs of infiltration or sabotage
- Gravity controls

## Life on the Shards

Each Shard is a melting pot of cultures, localised to the nearby nations of the space elevator below. The stations built upon the counter-weight asteroids for each elevator have evolved into micronations, each with its own distinct identity and governance structure. For those fortunate enough to secure passage to one of the eight Shards, life presents a stark contrast to the horrors of Errack's surface. However, this escape comes at a price: a life of struggle and hard work, all in service of the hope to one day reclaim what was once theirs on the planet below.   Citizens undergo regular defence drills and basic combat training. Public spaces double as fortified shelters, and even children learn to don pressure suits at a moment's notice. Despite the oppressive security measures, the people of the Shards have adapted, finding ways to express their humanity through art, culture, and community. Murals depicting heroic last stands against Myrkild attacks adorn reinforced walls, while communal spaces host regular "safety fairs" that combine entertainment with crucial survival skills.  

### Employment and Society

The Shards offer a wide array of employment opportunities, each with its own risks and rewards, for example:

- Filter scrubbers maintaining life support systems
- Ship breakers salvaging derelict spacecraft
- Miners extracting resources from nearby asteroids
- Pirates preying on vulnerable trade routes
- Technicians maintaining the crucial space elevators
- Scientists pushing the boundaries of survival technology

  The old saying, _"Out of the frying pan, into the fire"_, aptly describes the transition from Errack's surface to life on the Shards. While safer from the planet's immediate dangers, residents find themselves navigating the threat of their peers, a complex web of corporate interests, criminal organisations, and government agencies.  

### Culture and Aspirations

Despite the challenges, a unique culture has flourished on the Shards. Art, music, and literature reflect the dual nature of their existence: the struggle for survival and the dream of a better future. Many residents hold onto the hope of one day returning to a restored Errack, while others have embraced their new lives among the stars. Regular shuttles between the Shards foster a sense of unity among the orbital communities, while also highlighting the disparities between the Majoris and Minoris stations. This delicate balance of cooperation and competition drives innovation and ensures the continued survival of humanity above Errack.
