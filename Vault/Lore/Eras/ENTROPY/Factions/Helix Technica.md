Subsidised by the Imperium Coalition to research biotechnologies and demonic abominations - Helix Technica are the largest biotech corporation in the world, with their main headquarters bing located in Kisara.   With facilities to study and test both arms and armour against various strains of Demons, Helix Technica have been able to make great advancements for humanity throughout the Endless war.  

## Research history

Expanding on centuries of research and developments by nations and private organisations prior, Helix Technica are now at the forefront of biological and technological interfacing and enhancements.   The prestige of being the top-dog bio-technical company comes at a cost and for Helix Technica, that cost is decades of brutal testing and morally bankrupt practices all whilst kneecaping the competition with insider spying and legal actions.  

### NeMeSIS program

> Welcome, dear shareholders, to our newest project's kickoff! Without further ado, I present to you the New Medically and Surgically Improved Soldier program, or as we like to call it, the _Nemesis_ Program!

Founded to research and develop the most effective soldier possible to engage in deep strike missions within Nadir and Zenith territories, the NeMeSIS program was born.  
It would go on to make several itterations of it's AESIR _(Augmented and Enhanced Strike, Infiltration and Reconisance)_ soldier, in parternship with The Black Foundry, each time implementing new biological and technological enhancements to make the soldier increasingly effective and efficient to produce.

## Creations

### AESIR Mk.I

Developed under the NeMeSIS program, the AESIR Mk.I (Mk.1) soldier was conceptualised based upon the requirements of the Imperium Coalition's ICDF and ICAV. The two military organisations put out a joint request for a highly specialised soldier who would be able to participate in deep strike opperations with extreme effectiveness and leathality.   The result of this request was Helix Technica's first NeMeSIS soldier platform, the AESIR Mk.I.  
The AESIR Mk.I required multiple surgeries and medicinal concotions to alter the subject's biology enabeling for the core genetic enhancements that the NeMeSIS program was founded upon.   Mk.I AESIR soldiers were not provided the N.E.R.V system, however, the Mk.IIs would recieve it's enhancements.  

### N.E.R.V system

The N.E.R.V system allows for the direct and instantaneous interfacing of soldiers to their gear. Though not widely adopted and greately frowned upon due to the methodology required to implement such a system, N.E.R.V allows the user to respond faster and with greater agility than any other interfacing platform. As such, the AESIR Mk.IIs would all receive the N.E.R.V system to greater enhance their capabilities over the Mk.Is.
