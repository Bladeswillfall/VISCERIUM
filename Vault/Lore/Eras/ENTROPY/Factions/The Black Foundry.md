The Black Foundry, once known as The Black Armoury was founded in [1710], with a history dating back into the early 1300's.   Today, the Black Foundry are known for their role in equipping elite units throughout history with the best-of-the-best, cutting edge equipment for their specialised missions and roles. They are routinely the most advanced manufacturers of arms and armour year-on-year.   The Black Foundry are chartered by the Imperium Coalition to produce specialised equipment for special forces units. As such, they opperate under near limitless budgets and are void of bureaucratic restrictions.  

## History

The Black Foundry was formed upon the shoulders of the Black Armoury. The BA (Black Armoury) focused on weapons manufacturing and development - honing and refining new firearms to out-perform their mass-produced kin. In the early 1700's, the Black Foundry was formed, combining the Black Armoury's knowledge of blackpowder firearms and the Wassermann family's expertise in crafting the finest armours of the time.  
This would allow the Black Foundry to become a one-stop shop for the richest nations and most powerful businesses to arm and armour their kinsmen in the finest equipment money can buy.

### Bugblaster rounds

One of BF's most notable inventions of the recent few centuries was the invention of their _Bugblaster_ round - a variety of airbust round that is set to detonate directly infront of incoming targets - causing them to fly through a dense swarm of shrapnel. This inovative ammunition has proven extremely effective against both small and large demonic targets alike.
