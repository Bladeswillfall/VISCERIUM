The Imperium Coalition's standardised drop pod used by both the Imperium Coalition Astral Vanguard and Imperium Coalition Defence Force alike.   This vaccum to surface, single-personel transportation vehicle can be fired from both stations and vaccum-capable ships alike.  
Utilising a multi-stage design, the IC's drop pods have break-away paneling that activates during entry (just prior to engagement range) as to provide cover for the vehicle and it's occupant in the form of a shrapnel cloud.   For deceleration, four high-power thrusters work in tandem to bring the vehicle to a safe speed for impact via suicide burn. This ensures that the drop pod touches-down at the fastest possible rate avoiding as much enemy fire as feasible.
