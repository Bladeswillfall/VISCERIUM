The larger of [[Lore/Degel System/Errack]]'s twin moons, the eye of Vordr is named after Vordr (the Warden), whilst the eye of Visi is the namesake of Visi (the Sage). This is because when the two are near eachother it looks as though someone is watching you from the sky.   Vordr is the god of; Order, vigilance, protection, ambition, innovation, foresight, and war.  
Visi is the god of; Justice, adaptability, prudence, empathy, harmony, memory, and law.   Vordr and Visi are siblings of balance, it is believed that they are two halves of the same whole. Once a powerful being now seperated.   Vordr has a mostly iron-nickel alloy, gold, and tungsten core that also contains trace amounts of carbonaceous materials, copper and silicate inclusions, elements shared with its sibling moon, Visi. Its silicate mantle is mostly comprised of feldspar, quartz, mica, and amphibole. This unique composition gives the moon an overall red-brown, grey-white, and black appearance with the occasional flecks of gold.
