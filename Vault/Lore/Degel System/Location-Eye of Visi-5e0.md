The smaller of [[Lore/Degel System/Errack]]'s two moons. The Eye of the Sage.   Visi has a mostly compressed-carbon core, with trace amounts of radioactive isotopes and rare earth elements also present. Its composition also includes silicate inclusions, iron, nickel, and copper - elements also found on Vordr but in vastly different proportions. Most of its surface is a fractured mosaic of dark blacks and shimmering blue-greens. Veins of graphite and carbon crisscross regions where cryovolcanism sends plumes of ice crystals into the void, catching the light and creating an ethereal glow in the shadows. A veneer of fine dust, a mixture of those same silicates, carbonaceous compounds, and ice particles, blankets the surface. This dust, forming a neutral tan colour, softens the stark contrasts between the dark graphite and bright ice. Ice permeates and persists across the dark areas of Visi's surface in the perpetual shadows.
