> [!caution]
> This page is deliberately **non-canon test content**. The background is the real CITADEL map of Errack; the demo markers and relationships overlaid on it are not canon.

## What to test

Use the layer control, search for the demo locations, click markers for cards, and zoom in/out to confirm zoom-gated markers appear and disappear. The world background should render from the managed WebP asset rather than the synthetic SVG fixture.

The **Demo Gate City** marker opens a nested city map.
